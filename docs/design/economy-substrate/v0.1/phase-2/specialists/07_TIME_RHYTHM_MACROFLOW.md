T-01 examines selected monthly paid primary-earner households using 1986–March 1998 UK expenditure diaries. Its comparison accounts for calendar-month patterns; this supports investigating a liquidity rhythm separately from a general monthly seasonal effect. Weekly observations, selected households and historical institutions limit transferability. It supplies no evidence that every worker spends immediately or that drug, vice or robbery activity must rise. See [author paper §§1–3](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/mstep/wp-content/uploads/sites/930/2021/06/paydate_finalsub.pdf).

T-02 reports more leisure/socializing on average weekend days and less work overall, while many employed people still work weekends. This supports differentiated authored schedules. It does not justify closing every workplace or multiplying all demand together. See [Tables 2/4 and Technical Note](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/atus_06262025.htm).

T-03 demonstrates that even simple repeated transfers require stated assumptions and an activation policy. Our inference is to expose ordering and boundary assumptions in later paper cases; executable scheduling choices remain outside this phase. See [Mesa model description and activation example](https://mesa.readthedocs.io/stable/tutorials/0_first_model.html).

An additional lead, Guillaume Pierre's GDC 2024 [city-simulation slides](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2024/Slides/GDC%2Bslide%2Bpresentations/GDC24_GuillaumePierre_CitySimulation_SimCity_Cityscapes.pdf), returned HTTP 403; its [official catalogue](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1034347/We-Built-This-City-on) redirected to maintenance. ## 4. Documentary cases and boundary behavior

These are proposed reasoning exercises, not executed simulations or evidence of balance/playability. Numbers are illustrative.

| Case | Required distinction and candidate response | Unresolved choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payroll funding → wages → spending | A business withdraws 300 from bank to payroll custody. Its bank position falls while cash changes location. Paying 200 transfers that portion to workers; 100 remains. Workers' later purchases are separate effects. Neither the withdrawal nor payday creates another 300 or pays the same wage twice. | Funding failure, partial payment, payroll eligibility and fulfillment credit; roles 5/9. |
| Missed opening and catch-up | An authored 09:00 opening is an expectation. A known closure until noon excludes ordinary walk-in sales before noon. Advancing to 18:00 cannot charge for all nine planned trading hours. Previously represented hours cannot run again. | Ordinary background availability assumptions, partial intervals and bounded catch-up policy; roles 3/10. |
| Closed intervals | A closed shop can still owe rent, retain payroll cash or receive an authorized delivery; these are separate selected performances. Closure suppresses the applicable retail channel, not every economic effect. | Which channels remain possible and who may authorize receipt. |
| Explicit Player sale inside aggregate commerce | A one-item Player sale is excluded from the anonymous-sales coverage that concerns the same stock/time scope. Both inventory reduction and associated revenue count once. | Demand displaced by explicit sales versus additional demand; roles 3/4/5. |
| Shipment robbed during coarse delivery | A consequential interruption separates completed movement from unperformed delivery. Store replenishment cannot silently complete the whole shipment afterward. Promotion preserves retained origin, quantity, custody and effects; it cannot create an exact route, witness or departed-driver identity absent prior support. | Partial delivery/interruption ordering and supported retained detail; roles 4/9/10. |
| Busy club, slow pharmacy | Friday nightlife can increase a club's prospective traffic while a nearby pharmacy is closed or quiet. Staffing/stock limits prevent automatic conversion of every desired visit into revenue. | Local condition duration, correlation and authored response; roles 3/6/11. |

For a shipment due at 10:00, interrupted at 09:45, reported at 11:00 and discovered by another actor tomorrow, the deadline, occurrence, communication reception and discovery are distinct temporal anchors. A late report changes neither the earlier occurrence nor the deadline. A known payroll schedule does not disclose an actual withdrawal. [KW timing/performance](../../../../keywrapping-substrate/v0.1/TRANSACTIONS_COMMITMENTS.md) and [promotion/acquisition separations](../../../../keywrapping-substrate/v0.1/REFERENCES_PROMOTION.md) constrain these cases without freezing date fields or deadline policies.

## 5. Alternatives, content choices and unknown past

Always simulating individual shoppers would retain more detail at substantial cost and exceed the desired scope. Independently sampling each current business snapshot is cheaper but can contradict already committed stock, cash or deliveries. Prefer investigating bounded aggregate periods with explicit interruptions/exclusions; the engineering choice and resource envelope remain open.

Promotion during a lifecycle should continue the retained aggregate facts at a supported resolution. It must not replay earlier effects, refund earlier sales or retrospectively populate precise actors/events. If only a daily quantity is supported, the exact prior hour/customer remains unknown. Prospective detail may begin at promotion. Any necessary earlier detail requires an independently supported retained basis; otherwise expose the limitation and defer the consequential question. No new unlimited retention, compaction or reconstruction guarantee is proposed.

Content choices include opening hours, cross-midnight service, employer payday staggering, recurring cost cadence, demand profiles, restock intervals, capacity and player-facing opportunities. Exact rates, grace periods, holidays, time compression, simultaneous-event rules and fluctuation amplitudes are unselected. Clocks/timezones in source studies do not select Encounter's calendar. Gameplay notice must have a legitimate acquisition path; no automatic secret-schedule quest marker follows a privileged schedule.
