# Scope and subsystem boundary map

**Version:** v0.1 / Phase 1  
**Date:** 2026-10-23, America/New_York  
**Status:** PHASE 1 FRAMING FOR OWNER REVIEW; ECONOMY DESIGN NOT ACCEPTED

The research mission is to find the smallest causal economic grammar that creates material pressure, social opportunity, traceable value movement and recurring rhythms. Phase 1 identifies questions and boundaries; selection of primitives and mechanisms belongs to later authorized work.

## Economy scope to investigate later

| Area from Economy Context | Questions within the prospective Economy remit | Boundary to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player maintenance and housing | Material costs, access to food/sleep/hygiene, clothes, medicine, phone, housing, rent and upkeep | No survival/medical simulator; no new authoritative health or social-cognition owner |
| Businesses and employment | Shared concerns across stores, services, bars/clubs, warehouses, jobs, wages and ownership interests | Business configuration does not define universal person, group, authority or contract semantics |
| Goods and logistics | Stock, custody, deliveries, source/destination and meaningful item/shipment continuity | No national supply-chain simulation or identity for every background product |
| Money and recurring flows | Physical cash locations, bank balances, wages, expenses, revenue, payouts and recurring obligations | No selected banking implementation, globally balanced macroeconomy or complete accounting system |
| Records and fraud | Actual economic effects versus official/off-books descriptions, claims and discrepancies | Neither ledger is a truth owner; hidden-record existence does not make it discoverable |
| Legitimate and shadow activity | Investigate shared machinery for trade, illicit goods, fencing, vice and concealed revenue | No independent crime engine, operational real-world crime instructions or automatic enforcement |
| Time and fluctuation | Daily schedules, payday/weekend pulses, recurring costs and local variation | Encounter owns time; no separate authoritative clock, scheduler or selected rates |
| Aggregate/explicit activity | What must retain continuity when coarse commerce becomes consequential? | No invented precise past, double counting or assumption that general elaboration is implemented |
| Social capability | How employment, presentation, venues and property access create opportunities | No universal persuasion score, automatic knowledge sharing or finalized status threshold |

These rows preserve current design direction from Economy Context sections 1-57 and runbook sections 1, 5-26. They are an assignment scope, not an accepted Economy ontology or content catalog.

## Accepted KeyWrapping constraints to carry into research

The following grouping maps existing KW-01 through KW-15 to Economy concerns; it introduces no additional KeyWrapping invariant.

| Existing invariant references | Consequence for Economy questions |
| --- | --- |
| KW-01, KW-02, KW-04 | A recognizable business/item/deal reference must not disclose hidden ownership, aliases, records or linked identities |
| KW-03, KW-06 | Actual transfer, received evidence, claim, belief, reference and Player availability remain distinct |
| KW-05, KW-07, KW-15 | Economic significance, enduring continuity, active attention and acquired knowledge are separate; bounded elaboration does not promise unlimited history |
| KW-08, KW-09 | Economy must not add a Player reference-authoring UI or silently expand/freeze vocabulary and action senses |
| KW-10, KW-13 | Asking for payment, agreeing, becoming due, paying and crediting performance are different concerns; later disputes preserve prior effects |
| KW-11, KW-12 | Economic dialogue and outward cues cannot invent binding terms or expose sealed private reasoning |
| KW-14 | Nested links require bounded, audience-permitted exploration; economic provenance is not an omniscient traversal path |

## Shared gaps and decision routing

Use the runbook's marker **PROMOTE / RECONCILE / SHARED CONTRACT REQUIRED** when a question changes general ownership, authority, obligation, observation, identity, time or persistence semantics. Record the impacted accepted constraints, the concrete economic case and what remains undecided in the [conflict register](CONFLICT_REGISTER.md).

Economy may later propose economic specialization within those constraints. A specialist's preferred answer does not close a shared gap. Real-world research supplies analogy/evidence; it cannot select a game jurisdiction or govern Encounter.

## Phase 1 exclusions and completion boundary

This phase excludes external domain research, specialist substrate design, candidate data structures, executable schemas, APIs, migrations, runtime code, scenario execution, balancing, integration and product remediation. Research assignments are queued only. No Phase 2 assignment is dispatched by writing it down.

Phase 1 ends when the owner receives these maps, the queued assignments, the conflict register and bounded review evidence. Proceeding requires a new owner instruction. Stage 13 remains separately gated.
