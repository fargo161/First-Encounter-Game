# D01-D11 residual owner decision register

**Status:** PHASE 10 OWNER REVIEW PACKAGING / ALL ELEVEN RESIDUAL CARDS OPEN / NO POLICY INSTALLED

This is a concise routing view of the unchanged [Phase 4 reconciled register](../phase-4/RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md#L6). "Fixed" below means either an explicitly identified accepted external constraint or a selected separation in the proposed Economy candidate. Candidate selection is not final Economy acceptance, shared Governance ratification or implementation authorization. The [cold verification](../phase-9/OPEN_DECISION_VERIFICATION.md#L3) found no silently installed residual default; this package does not install one.

No entire card must be resolved merely to accept the bounded architectural/design substrate. All eleven can safely retain residual choices after such acceptance. Later work may proceed only within its separately authorized scope and may not claim an outcome that depends on an absent choice or contract. A local illustrative fixture can state its assumptions without adopting them as universal game policy.

## Routing categories

| Category | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A - Open after substrate acceptance | The residual can remain explicit without blocking architectural/design acceptance. Applies to every card. |
| B - Specific implementation/proof prerequisite | The named subchoice must be supplied before a dependent outcome, guarantee or executable proof can be claimed. It is not a blanket implementation authorization. |
| C - Later gameplay/content tuning | Owner-selected scope, values, cadence, progression or incentives; documentary coherence does not choose or validate them. |
| D - Other subsystem / Governance contract | Another authority or shared contract must provide the stated basis/support; Economy cannot decide it alone. |

Cards can span categories. The labels below identify which residual subchoices fall into each one; they are routing aids, not new architectural decisions or added acceptance conditions.

## D01 - Duty basis, allocation, priority and remedies

**Already fixed:** proposed F3 exact requirements/due/qualification/credited scope refer to the same F2 occurrence. Partial, late and disputed dimensions remain independent; same required scope cannot receive duplicate credit. Accepted KeyWrapping terms/authority/performance constraints apply. This selects neither universal assent for statutory duties nor generic overlap/priority rules.

**Still open / owner:** owner and shared Governance/KeyWrapping supply activation, allocation/reallocation authority, cross-duty overlap, priority, cancellation, substitution and remedies; Laws supplies any statutory basis.

**Recommended default, not installed:** explicit duty-specific basis and disjoint allocation in the first bounded fixture. Explicit shared credit/netting remains compatible if exact terms and authority support it. Disjoint allocation is simpler but narrower; richer settlements need precise overlap/priority meaning.

**May proceed without resolution:** separately authorized documentary reasoning about unpaid/partial/late duties and duplicate-credit rejection, while unknown activation or remedy stays unknown. **Required before a dependent claim:** exact supplied basis, eligible scope, allocation authority and remedy before declaring activation, discharge, cross-duty priority or remedy; executable same-occurrence support before a runtime guarantee.

**Routing:** A all residuals; B activation/credit/priority/remedy in a named case; C selected contractual content; D shared formation/allocation and Laws bases. [Current authority and recommendation](../phase-4/RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md#L12); [cold check](../phase-9/OPEN_DECISION_VERIFICATION.md#L9).

## D02 - Property, grants, interests and distribution

**Already fixed:** proposed F1 keeps economic interest, custody, possession, practical control and applicable grants separate. It references supported grants and cannot create authority. Operation, ownership and management differ. Profit, report, authorized distributable pool, funding and actual payout differ. Accepted Encounter/KeyWrapping authority constraints exclude automatic power from mere possession, payment or a share.

**Still open / owner:** owner/Governance choose entitlement-change timing, standing, transport/spend/manage/delegate/revoke scope, return/diversion basis, profit recognition and distribution authority.

**Recommended default, not installed:** economic participation with separately stated management grants. Explicitly bundled ownership packages remain possible if each power is defined. Separation limits accidental powers; bundling may serve intended gameplay but cannot infer collective assent or knowledge.

**May proceed without resolution:** documentary comparison of investor/manager/custodian relations and funded effects with dependent rights withheld. **Required before a dependent claim:** selected title timing, actual grants, recognition/pool/approval basis and funding before asserting ownership transfer, authority, entitlement or distribution.

**Routing:** A all residuals; B rights/powers/result/pool in a named proof; C selected ownership/distribution content; D standing and shared grant/authority support. [Current authority and recommendation](../phase-4/RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md#L20); [cold check](../phase-9/OPEN_DECISION_VERIFICATION.md#L10).

## D03 - Finite support and historical precision

**Already fixed:** finite supported scope and applicable protected causal support; no invented past, indefinite-history guarantee or presumed-safe deletion/compaction. Resource refinement is no new history. Accepted bounded C1 qualifications and KeyWrapping retention/acquisition separations constrain the proposal; neither supplies a general Economy capacity mechanism or permanent learned-book guarantee.

**Still open / owner:** owner/Core/Governance choose duration, activity, precision, supported questions and authorized behavior at the support limit; separately authorize and prove compaction if desired.

**Recommended default, not installed:** bounded support without compaction; decline/stop unsupported continuation before needed support is lost. This limits retrospective breadth. Later compaction could extend scale only with proof preserving required questions, not a summary assertion.

**May proceed without resolution:** identify support requirements and unavailable precision in finite documentary cases without claiming a runtime budget. **Required before a dependent claim:** selected support envelope/limit response before executable capacity or campaign-support claims; an actual preservation proof before compaction claims.

**Routing:** A envelope/limit/compaction choice; B capacity, repeat-prevention and safe compaction claims; C owner-selected breadth; D Core/Governance retention and shared support. [Current authority and recommendation](../phase-4/RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md#L26); [cold check](../phase-9/OPEN_DECISION_VERIFICATION.md#L11).

## D04 - Normative coverage and tax basis

**Already fixed:** economic fact, assertion, normative basis, valid authorized assessment, duty and payment differ. Cash surplus/commercial result is not automatically tax base. A discrepancy establishes no intent, classification, investigation or enforcement. Accepted subsystem and KeyWrapping actuality/claim/authority boundaries apply.

**Still open / owner:** owner/Laws/Governance select fictional rule authority, applicability, tax/reporting basis, due/assessment criteria and supported or unknown mental/permission facts.

**Recommended default, not installed:** narrow supplied fictional coverage where a later case needs it; omit rule-dependent outcome elsewhere. This enables bounded examples without importing real-world law, generalizing jurisdiction or guessing intent.

**May proceed without resolution:** documentary representation of actual receipts, reports, payments and disparities without declaring a tax liability, fraud or response. **Required before a dependent claim:** actual fictional normative/assessment/duty basis and supporting facts before classifying conduct, calculating liability or claiming a rule-dependent outcome. Laws/Information support remains necessary for any later applicable response.

**Routing:** A residual coverage; B normative/tax outcome in a named proof; C game-rule/rate selection; D Laws/Governance and any later response contract. [Current authority and recommendation](../phase-4/RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md#L32); [cold check](../phase-9/OPEN_DECISION_VERIFICATION.md#L12).

## D05 - Insurance recovery, salvage and repayment

**Already fixed:** actual loss, claim, coverage decision, funded payout and recovered original goods remain distinct. Recovery neither clones goods, reverses history, establishes repayment/salvage rights nor proves initial fraud. F1/F2/F3/F5 reuse preserves the actual trace.

**Still open / owner:** owner/Governance and relevant normative authority supply claim standing, coverage/limits, disclosure, recovery interests and any repayment trigger.

**Recommended default, not installed:** explicit policy-specific terms; no universal selection between no recovery-triggered repayment and explicit new disclosure/repayment/interest changes. Both preserve the trace but yield different rights/incentives; the latter needs an additional duty/authority basis.

**May proceed without resolution:** compare both variants as documentary challenges, leaving rights-dependent results conditional. **Required before a dependent claim:** selected coverage/standing/decision and recovery terms before asserting entitlement, salvage, disclosure or repayment; actual funded effect and repeat support before payout behavior claims. A policy-permitted payout-plus-recovery windfall is not itself duplicate value.

**Routing:** A remaining policy; B named claim/recovery rights or proof; C coverage, limits and incentives; D applicable normative/property/authority support. [Current authority and recommendation](../phase-4/RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md#L38); [cold check](../phase-9/OPEN_DECISION_VERIFICATION.md#L13).

## D06 - Household recovery and legitimate progression

**Already fixed:** Encounter supplies condition truth. Access, purchase, use and result differ; housing is a selected service/access bundle. Owner-established basic/emergency/simple-instruction/refusal exceptions exclude universal presentation lockout. Architectural representation establishes no health rate, recovery guarantee, balance or injury outcome.

**Still open / owner:** owner chooses reliable recovery floor versus terminal deprivation, legitimate progression scope/pace, failure policy and passive ownership growth.

**Recommended default, not installed:** modest reliable recovery and meaningful legitimate progression with authored time/quality/cost tradeoffs. Terminal deprivation remains an intentional alternative, not an architecture failure by itself; broader free support/growth reduces pressure. Recovery routes require actual content.

**May proceed without resolution:** document goods/service/access/use chains and compare conditional recovery paths without claiming viability or fun. **Required before a dependent claim:** supplied recovery/failure/progression policy and content before claiming a guaranteed route; later experience evidence before pressure, balance, playability or fun claims. External condition/injury outcome authority still applies.

**Routing:** A recovery/progression residuals; B a named recovery/progression proof; C recovery floor, needs rates, failure and passive growth; D only where condition/outcome integration is claimed. [Current authority and recommendation](../phase-4/RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md#L44); [cold check](../phase-9/OPEN_DECISION_VERIFICATION.md#L14).

**Still open / owner:** owner selects eligible acquired-context display or omission and bounded device storage/access/availability expectations; relevant Governance supplies shared support.

**Recommended default, not installed:** display only if needed, otherwise omit. Display can aid recall but needs exact eligible access; omission narrows scope. Broader scratchpad authoring requires separately authorized KeyWrapping architectural change, not merely a D07 toggle or another KeyWrapping acceptance record.

**May proceed without resolution:** document household access and eligible acquired fragments without choosing a phone feature. **Required before a dependent claim:** selected display/storage/access scope and support before asserting device behavior or eligible actor projection; separate architectural authorization for incompatible broader authoring.

**Routing:** A display/omission and device expectations; B a concrete display/projection proof; C feature/content choice; D shared acquisition support or any separately authorized KeyWrapping architecture change. Supported actual source, asserted source, evidence availability and actor acquisition remain separate. Generic cleanliness is excluded, not awaiting creative selection.

## Owner decision scope

These are preserved residuals, not manufactured acceptance conditions, defects or instructions to implement. Phase 5, Phase 7 and Phase 9 review evidence did not close them. Accepting a bounded design baseline could preserve all eleven cards exactly as open while requiring dependent future work to name its local choices and authorities. Only the owner may accept that baseline or supply conditions. No condition is supplied on the owner's behalf here, and no broader subsystem decision is ratified by this register.
