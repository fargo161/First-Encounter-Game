## Joint recommendation: retain five families with mandatory semantic roles

The five-family organization is adequate as a documentary grouping **if the roles below remain distinguishable**. It is not evidence that five runtime classes, five stores or five services are appropriate. A new family would not itself protect a distinction; the audit candidate must state the role boundary directly. Conversely, preserving five names while using one all-purpose status would fail this reconciliation.

Do not make F1 a general permission engine. Reference applicable scoped grants rather than deriving them from holdings or shares. |

There is one actual occurrence/history authority. F2 is the economic specialization of admitted occurrences, not an independent authoritative event log. Record creation, alteration, delivery and destruction also occur under Encounter's authority. Their economic meaning may matter without making every record operation a separate economic resource transfer. F5 references that history; it does not replay or own it. A physical notebook's carrier can participate in F1 holdings when consequential, while F5 describes its contents; these are roles of the supported thing, not two notebooks.

An interest relation in F1 and its supplied basis in F3 are also different roles of the same supported relation, not two independent ownership balances. F4 may use current practical control/capability but must not maintain a competing control fact. Expected availability, exclusive allocation, an actual holding, a normative right and an actor's belief about them can coexist and disagree without contradiction.

### Authentic records and valid acts

Avoid the overbroad claim that all account content is causally inert. An authentic payslip saying paid does not pay wages. A false claim of authority does not create a grant. However, a separately supported current institutional act can form assent, a permission or another selected normative change when its exact sense, delivery, standing, target, time and lifecycle guards hold under accepted KeyWrapping C05/C11. A document can carry or evidence that act under a selected rule. Its effects arise from the valid act and basis, not from the truth of every assertion in the document.

For example, a competent grantor's actual bounded access grant can change the supported permission relation without opening a door or teaching its contents. An impostor's equally fluent statement leaves a real communication and possibly a received claim, but supplies no valid grant. The audience must not receive the hidden authority-check reason merely because the act failed: KeyWrapping C06 and P12/P14 still constrain feedback. Economy proposes no new validity algorithm or permit format.

## Agent 8: Laws / Regulation boundary findings

The useful four lanes from the specialist report remain irreducible: actual economic conduct; normative classification under a selected basis; acquired information/evidence; and institutional response. Combining them into F2, F3 or F5 must not imply that any lane causes the next automatically.

1. **Physical occurrence versus permission.** A forbidden transfer can physically occur and be admitted as a world consequence. Engine admission and normative permission are different meanings of authorization. Describe F2 as an actual supported effect, rather than implying every effect is lawful or the actor had social permission.
2. **Claimed rule versus applicable rule.** A shop notice, false tax demand or forged insurance policy can exist in F5. Whether a valid applicable rule creates a duty requires its supplied basis and standing. Official appearance does not fill the gap; an actor may nevertheless believe the claim.
3. **Rule evaluation versus allegation.** A supported normative evaluation, if later authorized, is not merely another person's belief. An actor's assessment is F5; an authoritative classification depends on the selected external rule authority and supported facts. Do not silently demote every valid institutional act to opinion or promote every official assessment to truth.
4. **Incomplete facts stay incomplete.** An aggregate outflow can establish that goods left a stock pool while failing to establish a named taker, exact minute, permission or intent. Future law cannot require invented facts to reach a verdict. An unresolved classification is a supported outcome of insufficient basis, not a documentary failure to be repaired by fabrication.
5. **Remedies require new causes.** A rule, claim, order or entitlement does not itself move money, repossess goods, injure an actor or teach an investigator. If a later scope authorizes a response, its admitted effect must be separately supported. No general violent-crime classification, enforcement, investigation, penalty or seizure engine belongs to Economy.

These findings support a compact economic substrate. They do not select a jurisdiction, tax basis, mental-element rule, consent policy, title-transfer rule or insurance-recovery right. The absence of a dedicated accepted Laws contract in the inspected material grants no authority to choose one here.

## Agent 9: traceability, acquisition and finite continuity findings

### Three provenance questions and two precision limits

Actual causal support, asserted provenance and actor-acquired provenance remain separate. A forged delivery receipt can have a real authoring/delivery history, falsely claim a shipment, and be received only in part. F5 must support all three facts without creating the alleged shipment, assuming the signer is the actual author, or revealing the hidden author. The actor's acquired source can be only the person who handed over the document, even if privileged retained history contains a longer path.

Retained world precision and acquired actor precision also differ. Retaining a coarse source category or quantity does not give every actor that category/quantity. Giving an actor a testimony containing a precise time does not make that time true. Conversely, actual provenance may be unavailable at the requested precision without implying no source existed.

Coarse aggregate support is legitimate when it suffices for the bounded consequence. It need not instantiate every buyer, banknote or product. Splitting a supported current pool can distinguish a consequential item prospectively while leaving its earlier precise ancestry unresolved. Mixing can retain supported source contributions without promising an exact note-to-source mapping. It must preserve any consequential distinctions already guaranteed by the declared support; it cannot erase them simply by naming a larger pool.

### Coverage is neither another effect nor retrospective repair

Coverage answers which activity/resource/time scope has already contributed to the actual positions/effects. It is a support requirement of F1/F2, not a sixth state owner. Increasing detail cannot be paid for with another stock decrement or revenue increment. In a shipment of ten, two already received and three later diverted leave five for possible subsequent delivery, under the supplied order; the original ten cannot arrive again.

For an aggregate period already containing a coarse sale, an explicit description can refine or associate only what the retained basis supports. If an explicit purchase is genuinely additional, its coverage must be outside the earlier scope. The policy deciding whether Player trade displaces anonymous demand remains content; either policy must satisfy no-overlap. This is a semantic requirement, not an allocation algorithm or ordering protocol.

### Finite history is a cross-family precondition

The retained-support envelope must state duration/activity scale, the questions/precision it promises, protected causal history, active and historically consequential duties, previous credit/retry identity, and aggregate exceptions. A closed duty, an archived ledger, loss of active attention, destroyed evidence or a derived summary does not authorize deletion of applicable protected history. Actor memory/availability and actual reception history remain different from current access.

The default recommendation remains a finite first proof with applicable protected causal prefix and no compaction assumption. This is compatible with C1's qualifications; it does not generalize C1's measured profile into Economy capacity. Capacity values and a supported response before required support is lost remain owner/Core choices. The documentary safety requirement is that unsupported continuation cannot silently proceed by forgetting an effect, exact term, adverse consequence or same-occurrence credit basis. No storage threshold, failure code, pruning mechanism or unlimited campaign promise is supplied.

2. **Material change and mixed provenance:** selected consumption, spoilage/transformation, pool split/merge and consequential-item refinement. 4. **Service occurrence and performance credit:** actual work/service, exact beneficiary/quality/time criterion, partial credit, and an independent disclosure requirement.
