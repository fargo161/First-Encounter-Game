# Unresolved owner/Governance decisions

**Status:** RECOMMENDATIONS FOR REVIEW / NO SHARED CHOICE INSTALLED

These eleven cards isolate decisions that the Economy synthesis cannot accept on another authority's behalf. Their defaults make a concrete future slice discussable; they do not authorize implementation or require all answers before owner review of Phase 3. All other in-scope selections appear in the [conflict resolutions](CONFLICT_RESOLUTIONS.md). A later phase may refine the recommendations, but cannot treat silence as acceptance.

## D01: Duty basis, allocation, priority and remedies

**Sources:** P2-Q01/Q07, with tax Q08. **Decision authority:** owner and shared semantic Governance/KeyWrapping; Laws for statutory basis.

- **Alternatives:** A universal negotiated contract with uniform activation/credit/remedies; B differentiated wage, household, commercial and statutory formation bases sharing exact performance concerns; C separate duty engines for each domain.
- **Recommended default:** B. Supply each fixture's exact duty basis, due condition and partial scope; use explicit disjoint payment allocation to duties, with no implicit cross-duty credit or priority. Preserve accepted KeyWrapping terms/performance separations.
- **Tradeoffs:** B preserves meaningful formation differences and reuse but requires explicit case terms. A falsely treats statutory duties as assent. C duplicates credit/payment behavior. Explicit allocation is narrower than a general netting system.
- **Specific decision required:** approve permitted formation/activation bases, who may allocate/reallocate performance, overlapping-credit conditions if desired, priority/cancellation and any remedy semantics. Decide which remain fixture content and which need shared rules. Required before claiming those generic rules or running a case whose result depends on them; not authority to implement them.

## D02: Property, grants, interests and distribution powers

**Sources:** P2-Q02/Q03. **Decision authority:** owner and Governance for shared standing/authorization; Encounter retains admitted consequences.

- **Alternatives:** A possession/payment automatically determines title and shares bundle control; B interests, custody/control and grants are separately supported under scoped terms/rules; C avoid all ownership changes and business interests in the first slice.
- **Recommended default:** B, with economic participation only unless another action grant is explicit. First cases state entitlement timing and authority to move/distribute; a defined percentage applies to an approved distribution basis, never automatic gross receipts.
- **Tradeoffs:** B supports entrusted cash, prepayment, delayed delivery and hidden interests without new legal machinery, but needs more explicit fixtures. A creates unsupported permissions. C is simpler but misses core owner use cases.
- **Specific decision required:** choose entitlement-change standing/timing for sale/return/diversion; define transport/spend/manage/delegate/revoke scopes and who approves distributions. Do not infer faction assent or collective knowledge. Required before a future case relies on these powers.

## D03: Finite support envelope and historical precision

**Sources:** P2-Q05/Q06; C1 finite-support/full-prefix constraints. **Decision authority:** owner and Encounter/Core, with Governance for shared guarantees.

- **Alternatives:** A indefinite reconstructible campaign history; B finite supported duration/activity envelope preserving required causal prefix; C compaction with selected proven semantic guarantees.
- **Recommended default:** B for the first bounded consumer, with coarse pools plus explicit consequential exceptions and no compaction. At the declared limit, decline/stop unsupported continuation before losing required support. No numerical capacity or runtime response is selected here.
- **Tradeoffs:** B limits campaign breadth/retrospective precision but is compatible with existing finite proof constraints. A lacks support. C requires separately scoped proof of preservation and cannot be assumed from a summary.
- **Specific decision required:** declare duration, activity volume, supported questions/granularity, protected history/retry/credit needs, and permitted response at capacity. Decide whether any compaction research is wanted later. Required before claiming supported runtime duration, save/reload or retrospective guarantees.

## D04: Fictional normative coverage and tax basis

**Sources:** P2-Q08 plus Q01/Q02/Q16. **Decision authority:** owner, Laws/Regulation and Governance; not external law sources.

- **Alternatives:** A broad implicit jurisdiction and automatic classification; B narrow explicit fictional rule bases/coverage, with supported facts separate from assessment; C postpone all normative classification.
- **Recommended default:** B for later bounded documentary cases, C wherever no rule has been supplied. One authored tax/reporting basis can be specified separately from cash-flow operating result; no rate or law is imported by this proposal.
- **Tradeoffs:** B permits useful discrepancy/tax examples while avoiding a full legal system; coverage remains limited. A invents intent and enforcement. C cannot demonstrate rule-dependent duties.
- **Specific decision required:** identify rule authority, applicable activity/actor/time/resource scope, due/reporting/assessment basis, and which mental/permission facts are supported or unknown. Select whether any response is in a later authorized scope. Required before a case claims liability, theft, fraud, tax due or legal effect.

## D05: Insurance recovery, salvage and repayment

**Source:** P2-Q09. **Decision authority:** owner and Governance for supplied policy/duty/property semantics; Laws where normative effects are claimed.

- **Alternatives:** A automatic payout reversal/title reassignment on recovery; B explicit policy-specific coverage, disclosure, salvage and repayment terms; C loss/claim/payment examples without recovery rights.
- **Recommended default:** B. Retain actual loss, payout and recovery independently; any repayment is a new duty/payment and any salvage change needs its own supported basis.
- **Tradeoffs:** B handles honest delayed recovery and contested claims without rewriting history, but policy terms must be stated. A hides rights and ordering. C is narrower and omits a required combination.
- **Specific decision required:** choose who may claim, what is covered, payout limit/basis, recovery disclosure, recovered-property interest and repayment triggers. Required before testing entitlement or repayment, rather than merely trace continuity.

## D06: Household recovery and legitimate progression

**Source:** P2-Q10 and gameplay report. **Decision authority:** owner for content/failure policy; Encounter/Combat retain condition consequences and later play evidence must assess experience.

- **Alternatives:** A terminal deprivation is a normal first-slice outcome; B accessible modest recovery and viable legitimate progression with time/quality tradeoffs; C unrestricted free recovery and broad offscreen wealth growth.
- **Recommended default:** B. Include at least one authored basic recovery route, preserve the owner's basic/emergency presentation exceptions, and allow meaningful legitimate progression rather than survival alone.
- **Tradeoffs:** B reduces self-locking poverty traps while retaining costs; its minimum access must be authored. A can lock out work/services. C can remove pressure and local choices. None proves fun or determines injury rules.
- **Specific decision required:** choose guaranteed recovery access, terminal/failure conditions, contextual presentation application, progression ceiling/pace and permitted passive ownership growth. Required before claiming a balanced playable needs loop. Exact decay/rates/medical effects remain unselected.

## D07: Phone backlog versus authored notes

**Source:** P2-Q11. **Decision authority:** owner and KeyWrapping Governance for any reference-authoring change; Economy only proposes capability/access.

- **Alternatives:** A display eligible already acquired context; B freeform notes or reference-authoring scratchpad; C omit phone backlog in the first slice.
- **Recommended default:** A if needed, otherwise C; defer B. Accepted KW-08 constraints remain binding. Replacement follows supported storage/access and cannot reset learned knowledge or invent acquisition.
- **Tradeoffs:** A offers useful recall with limited utility and requires bounded display/access support. B has unresolved semantics and may conflict with accepted reference-authoring separation. C reduces scope.
- **Specific decision required:** select backlog/notes behavior and retention/access expectations, and explicitly commission reconciliation if broader authoring is desired. This is not a new prerequisite to accepting KeyWrapping architecture. Required before specifying that feature beyond display-only context.

## D08: Local rhythms, skipped time and anonymous demand

**Source:** P2-Q12 and coverage part of Q05. **Decision authority:** owner for gameplay/content policy; Encounter/Governance for any shared time/ordering guarantee.

- **Alternatives:** A synchronized universal payday/weekend boosts and automatic catch-up; B employer/local schedules with authored capacity-constrained conditions; C wholly reactive trade with no background rhythm.
- **Recommended default:** B with staggered paydays, differentiated local weekend/nightlife windows and no replay of covered periods. For the first numeric fixture explicitly exclude Player purchases from anonymous coverage; alternative displacement can be authored separately. Offscreen earning requires real supported inputs/availability/costs.
- **Tradeoffs:** B makes local timing readable without universal farming, but needs fixture choices. A risks implied extra revenue and guaranteed demand. C removes desired background life. Waiting for genuinely new supported earnings remains legitimate.
- **Specific decision required:** choose cadence, Player/anonymous demand displacement, missed-opportunity treatment, offscreen operation extent and skipped-time cost/earning policy. Required before a case asserts those counterfactual earnings or gameplay balance; no general scheduler protocol implied.

## D09: Service activities, outcomes and Combat facts

**Sources:** P2-Q14 plus Q08/Q10. **Decision authority:** owner for included content; appropriate Encounter/Combat/Laws authority for outcomes, consent/coercion and consequences.

- **Alternatives:** A include broad vice/gambling outcomes in Economy; B first prove ordinary service/access, defer specialized outcomes; C omit services and represent everything as goods.
- **Recommended default:** B. Keep reusable service performance, capacity, stakes/payment distinctions where supplied. No label supplies a gambling result, injury, consent or coercion.
- **Tradeoffs:** B proves reuse with less breadth. A exceeds current scope and manufactures missing authority. C misrepresents services and forces fake inventory.
- **Specific decision required:** select desired activities and identify the authorized source/basis of outcomes, participation/consent facts, interruptions and payouts. Required before designing/testing those specialized outcomes; ordinary service representation can proceed as a proposal now.

## D10: First bounded consumer and future shared contract

**Sources:** P2-Q04/Q05/Q15 plus repetition/ordering concerns. **Decision authority:** owner, Encounter/Core and relevant shared Governance/KeyWrapping/Information authorities.

- **Alternatives:** A immediate general Economy backend/shared schema; B one named bounded store-warehouse-household consumer, then review only required shared guarantees; C continue documentation without any consumer selection.
- **Recommended default:** B as a future scope proposal, with C until authorized. The proposed proof package identifies the concrete loop. No native M3/C1 example or reviewed C2.1 B0 is an installed bridge.
- **Tradeoffs:** B makes missing authority, ordering, identity, same-occurrence, acquisition and retention guarantees reviewable without freezing general APIs. A prematurely chooses integration. C preserves safety of scope but cannot demonstrate behavior.
- **Specific decision required:** name consumer/scenario, supported boundaries and existing native semantics it relies on; identify gaps needing shared review, then authorize that work separately. Required before claiming an integration contract, executable proof, crash/reload guarantee or actor-facing knowledge path. No implementation permission follows this recommendation.

## D11: Intended laundering/concealed-revenue benefit

**Source:** P2-Q16. **Decision authority:** owner for gameplay benefit, with Information/Laws/Governance for any future institutional decision.

- **Alternatives:** A no special laundering reward: ordinary supported access/spending, mixing and concealment; B a specified acquired report can influence a bounded counterparty/access decision; C generic laundering success/cleanliness changes source or automatic institutional acceptance.
- **Recommended default:** A for the minimum substrate; B only as later explicitly scoped content with a named recipient, acquisition and decision basis. Reject C as incompatible with preserved provenance and audience boundaries.
- **Tradeoffs:** A is coherent before future Information/Laws implementation but may provide less explicit progression payoff. B can supply payoff without changing source, but needs a real bounded consumer. C hides institutional/knowledge assumptions. Business size constrains real trade, never the magnitude of a false assertion.
- **Specific decision required:** choose A or a concrete B benefit and state present costs/access/funding/opportunity constraints. Do not count hypothetical future enforcement as an existing balancing cost. No selected benefit may erase retained source or grant automatic police knowledge.
