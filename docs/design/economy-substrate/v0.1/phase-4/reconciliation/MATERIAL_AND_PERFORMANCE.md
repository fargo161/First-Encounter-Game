## Family verdict: five envelopes with mandatory semantic roles

Retain five documentary envelopes for the audit candidate, while rejecting the interpretation that each is one interchangeable primitive or one runtime object. Five is a useful responsibility grouping, not a proven mathematical minimum.

| Envelope | Minimum owned economic meaning | Mandatory internal separations | Excluded or derived concern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 Holdings / Interests | Supported resource positions and economic relationships at declared precision | Resource kind; coarse position versus individually consequential item; physical placement/holding versus property/economic interest; possession, entrusted custody, practical control and scoped authorization when consequential; physical cash versus bank account position | Interest percentages are not extra goods quantities. Outstanding payment duties are not spendable cash. No group authority or legal standing is inferred. |
| F2 Economic Effects | Supported actual changes/performances concerning F1 resources and relevant operations | Money movement, goods movement, interest/grant change under its supplied basis, consumption/transformation/loss and service performance; occurrence versus acknowledgment; partial effects versus whole exchange | Does not decide whether the occurrence fulfills a duty, is legal, was observed or is a newly created historical occurrence merely because detail increased. |
| F3 Terms / Duties / Performance | Supplied basis, exact required scope and supported relation between qualifying F2 performance and fulfillment credit | Formation, activation, due condition, required scope, actual performance reference, qualification, credit and outstanding scope; different negotiated, wage, household and statutory bases | No second service/work/payment occurrence. No universal assent, priority, netting, remedy or debt lifecycle. |
| F4 Operations | Authored configurations plus supported operational availability, capacity, inputs and action scopes | Business configuration, expected schedule, actual availability, service capacity and resource/grant dependencies; owner versus operator versus employee | Opportunity is a derived conjunction of these facts, actor capability, time, access and supplied terms. Do not make an independent opportunity balance, guaranteed outcome or canonical social-access score. |
| F5 Attributed Accounts | Supported existence/authorship/content/change of accounts and assertions about economic matters | Record creation versus asserted event; official versus off-books context; claim versus authoritative assessment; account access versus actor acquisition | The account is not the actual holding/effect/duty it describes. A derived correct commercial-result view is not made fallible merely by living beside reported figures; an attributed report of that result can be false. |

The correction to F3 is substantive: **actual performance belongs to the same supported occurrence used by F2**. F3 qualifies/credits that occurrence against exact terms. Otherwise a meal service or wage payment could be instantiated once as an effect and again as a performance. Likewise F4 does not own a second access grant where F1 represents the supplied grant; F4 refers to that relevant scope.

Provenance, aggregate coverage, retained precision, finite support and audience separation constrain these meanings. They are not extra stores of truth. A resource position and the effects explaining its supported change can both be relevant facts without becoming independent writers. The future Encounter representation must establish their consistency; this memo selects no storage method.

Alternatives considered: split F1 into resources and interests, or split F3 into duties and fulfillment. Those would emphasize distinctions but add top-level families without resolving an authority or composition failure once mandatory roles are explicit. Collapsing F2 into F3 would erase effects with no outstanding duty, including gifts, consumption and unauthorized diversion. Collapsing F5 into F2 would confuse an actual statement with the event asserted by it. Collapsing F4 into F1 would conceal availability/capacity/service conditions. Retain the envelopes and make these prohibited shortcuts explicit.

## Agent 3 perspective: Business / Retail

### Operation, ownership and commercial result

A business operation is a configuration of premises, resources, staff, offerings, availability and permissions. Its existence neither creates a corporate person nor bundles shares, management, employment and Factions membership. An owner may lack current operational control; a manager may operate without economic participation; a cashier may possess a till without entitlement to its contents. Concealed ownership changes an interest's attribution/access, not the operation's physical resource rules.

Phase 3's cash-flow operating comparison is useful but **insufficient as the sole representation of business profit**. Preserve two separately declared derived views when the selected case needs both:

- Cash surplus/shortfall: included actual receipts less included actual payments for a declared interval/scope.
- Commercial result/profit: included recognized receipts/revenue and costs under an explicitly authored recognition basis. Unpaid earned costs and inventory timing may differ from cash movements. This is not adoption of real accounting standards or a general accrual engine.

An illustrative counterexample, **not run**: receipts are 60; goods used have an authored recognized cost of 20; workers have earned an unpaid wage cost of 10; the business pays 50 for replacement goods that remain in stock. Under those explicitly selected classifications, cash surplus is 10 while commercial result is 30. The 20 prior-stock cost must have a supplied opening basis. Restock payment is counted once as a cash outflow; it is not simultaneously charged again as the cost of goods used under this example's commercial basis. This example chooses neither a universal recognition rule nor a tax base.

Neither measure equals distributable return. Interest percentage, authorized distribution basis, reserves/constraints, approval, available funds and actual payment are separate. A profit report cannot mint cash; buying an existing owner's interest does not fund the operation unless that is what the supplied deal actually does. A concealed partner can have a real participation interest and receive a false statement about results without automatically learning the actual figures.

### Services, opportunities and access

Restaurants, bars, clubs, warehouses, rental homes and service businesses share F4 operational configuration. Merchandise, admission and service completion do not collapse: a cover payment can be real while admission remains unperformed, and admission can occur without an introduction or social assent. A staffing interruption can stop food service while rent, custody and existing duties persist.

An opportunity is a supported possibility, not an owned resource or successful interaction. Employment supplies a route to possible observation/work; it grants no closing-routine knowledge. A ban, the actor's communication of it, a clerk's recognition and physical refusal remain distinct. Scoped property/access authority is a supplied basis, not a new Economy policy-conflict winner.

## Agent 4 perspective: Inventory / Logistics

### Kind, stock and particular item

A product kind describes substitutability at selected precision. A quantity pool is actual supported stock. A consequential item needs individual continuity only when that distinction is retained/required. Three packages of a kind are not three extra quantities in addition to their parent pool. Refinement partitions the same supported quantity; representation-level regrouping neither produces goods nor performs delivery.

Inventory is a view of physical and relational facts. Physically present, held by a customer, reserved, exclusively allocated, in transit, expected and recorded are not interchangeable. A Player-held unit can remain subject to the store's interest while being unavailable to anonymous shelf sale. Exclusive allocation can constrain a supported portion without moving it; a promise without exclusive allocation is not stock creation.

Do not collapse possession and custody by definition. For a consequential fixture, a worker may immediately possess a parcel while a named operation/actor retains an entrusted custody responsibility under supplied terms. Practical control and permission must remain separate in either formulation. The generic meaning and authority for those relationships remains a shared question; no real legal responsibility is inferred from the word custody. Where only coarse holding is relevant, do not fabricate separate relationships merely to fill labels.

### Logistics and refinement

Payment for ten units, expectation, allocation, dispatch, transit holding, receipt and fulfillment credit are different concerns. If two units arrive and three of the eight remaining are diverted, later receipt can concern at most the supported five remaining units; a full-delivery manifest can still be false. This is a proposed semantic challenge, not a performed conservation audit. Any violent interruption is supplied aftermath from Combat/Encounter, not resolved here.

Coarse-to-explicit refinement needs supported source/resource/activity/time coverage and consequential exceptions. An aggregate receipt total need not produce a named customer per sale. A later disputed phone cannot acquire a fabricated exact buyer, serial or witness from its current significance. Supported linkage survives; unsupported linkage stays unresolved. A claim can allege a link without creating it. Mixing cannot erase a consequential difference already retained; it also cannot justify microscopic ancestry absent support.

## Agent 5 perspective: Money / Banking / Accounting / Fraud

### Money forms, movement and duty credit

Shared amount/form reasoning does not make a bank position a bag of cash at a virtual location. Physical cash has supported location/holder/custody. Bank money is a supported account position recognized by the selected institution/world facts, with separately supported access/control. A statement is an account about that position. A claimant's alleged balance and an unpaid deposit claim are not automatically recognized spendable balances.

Entrusted cash moves while the owner's economic interest can persist. Deposit receipt and bank credit can occur separately if the fixture supplies such terms. A delay/failure cannot leave the departed cash available to the payer and also invent a credited bank position. Banking conversion is not sales revenue, and payroll funding is not worker receipt. No general settlement protocol, bank credit creation or overdraft model follows.

The same 10-unit money movement could be purchase payment, wage payment, debt repayment, a gift, entrusted transfer or owner contribution. Its actual source/destination does not determine the commercial basis. Sale/gift/wage/repayment are distinct compositions or purpose relations where consequential, not four new movement primitives. A sale can require goods or service performance; a gift need not create reciprocal consideration; wages need the supplied eligibility basis; debt repayment credits exact outstanding scope. A promised gift can itself have supplied terms without turning all gifts into purchases. Incoming funds do not all become revenue.

F2 can represent an actual unauthorized diversion. Calling it unauthorized does not make the movement unreal. F3 separately asks whether an occurrence qualifies under the selected performance/authority terms. Permission, physical ability, actual occurrence, qualifying performance and legal classification must not be conflated by a general authorized-transfer shortcut.

### Records, claims and insurance

An actual record-writing occurrence differs from the sale asserted in it. Official and off-books accounts share F5; both may lie differently or omit facts. A claim is an assertion with an attributed source, not an authoritative loss, due amount, payout decision or investigator conclusion. Authentic authorship does not establish asserted truth. A correct derived value and a report of that value must remain distinguishable even when the report uses identical numbers.

Loss, claim, coverage decision, payout, recovered original goods, subsequent property disposition and any repayment are distinct. Payout is funded money movement; recovery concerns supported goods. A policy can make recovery trigger a new repayment/disclosure duty if its basis is supplied, but recovery cannot retroactively delete the payout or prove the original claim dishonest. Salvage/title changes need their own supported basis. No universal insurance reversal or second-payout rule is appropriate.

Fraud remains actual conduct plus attributed assertions and any separately supported authority/mental facts under supplied normative coverage. A stock discrepancy or no-show is not enough to infer intent. Real capacity constrains actual sales; it does not constrain the number someone can falsely write in a ledger. There is no truth validator, detector or clean-money transition in these families.
