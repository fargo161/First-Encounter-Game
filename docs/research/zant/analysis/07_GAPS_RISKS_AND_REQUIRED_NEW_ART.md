# Gaps, risks and required new art

The handoff is sufficient to choose a bounded standard and specify a proof. It is not a finished interchangeable body/clothing bank. The main production gap is authored, registered four-view/pose output with hidden continuation and verified interfaces. Preserve every supplied asset; author any future derivatives into a separately versioned workspace after owner approval.

This pass creates none of that new artwork. It identifies and bounds the future authoring need. Existing front art may reduce tracing/painting effort but cannot eliminate missing hidden surfaces, foreshortening, actual garment deformation or multi-view identity judgment.

## Decisions made versus decisions reserved for proof

Recommended now: original art outranks structured labels; one semantic alignment vocabulary with garment-aware authored whole-limb output; four canonical authoring views; explicit fit compatibility; view-specific five-slot implementation; controlled semantic material masks; known-good facial presets plus reviewed mixing; person-first creation; immutable source archive.

Reserve until proof evidence: permitted transform tolerances; exact useful pose count; need for extra shoulder/seat patches; numerical overlap margins at final display size; palette extremes; amount of separate gaze geometry; extended lower-face footprint by identity; permissible head/body facing offsets; reliable cross-fit ranges; long-garment states; production atlas/storage format. None of these requires redesigning Encounter or Room Creator.

## Owner review gate

The next decision is whether to authorize the single proof specification, including its limited new-art budget and pass/fail gates. Success should unlock the next one or two garment/fit difficulties, not conversion of all 44 characters. Failure should narrow or revise the standard with source fidelity as the judge. Full implementation, mass conversion, final artwork generation and integration remain outside this analysis approval.
