# Proposed unified Economy Substrate v0.1

**Status:** FIRST COHERENT SYNTHESIS / PROPOSED / NOT AN ACCEPTED SHARED CONTRACT  
**Authority:** Owner-authorized Phase 3 documentary design only, 2026-10-23

## 1. Selection and authority

Select five reusable families. Treat a store, warehouse, household, employer, fence or mixed-activity business as a configuration of those families. Treat a sale, wage payment, diversion, insurance recovery or false revenue report as distinguishable uses of them. Do not retain a separate engine for each specialist's label.

| Family | Minimum meaning | Distinctions preserved inside the family |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1: holdings and interests | Supported money/goods positions, their relevant relationships and scoped grants | Cash versus bank position; product kind versus quantity pool versus consequential item; property/economic interest versus custody, possession, practical control and authorized action |
| F2: economic effects | Actual supported movements, uses, transformations, losses and service performances | Source/destination, scope and causal support; monetary movement versus goods movement; genuine new occurrence versus repeated acknowledgment |
| F3: terms, duties and performance | Supplied exact basis and required scope, due conditions, actual qualifying performance and credited fulfillment | Agreement/formation versus obligation; work versus wage entitlement versus payment; performance versus credit; partial versus complete required scope |
| F4: operations and opportunities | Configurable commercial/household activity with offerings, inputs, availability, capacity, access and rhythm | Expected hours versus actual operation; resource/capability access versus successful use; local demand versus actual trade; services versus merchandise |
| F5: attributed accounts | Records, claims and reports with supported authorship, content, existence and access history | Assertion versus actuality; official versus private recording context; report versus assessment; record existence versus acquired actor knowledge |

F1's relationship distinctions are not collapsed into a single owner flag. Provenance and coverage qualify F1/F2; performance support qualifies F3; actor access and finite retained support constrain all five. Making these separate stores of economic truth would duplicate authority. The [mapping](PRIMITIVE_CONSOLIDATION_MAP.md) records every selection and rejection of a standalone primitive.

**Repository authority:** Encounter owns admitted gameplay identity, interaction meaning, validation, consequences, logical time and authoritative history. Economy proposes the meaning of economic facts to be represented under that authority; it acquires no independent mutation authority. Native reference scopes remain producer + exact package/artifact + local kind/ID, preserving native version/hash/frame boundaries. Atlas descriptive projections, RC spatial metadata and ZANT presentation do not establish economic truth or permissions. Stage 12 consolidation conventions remain documentary; Stage 13 is gated.

**Owner-established direction:** accepted KeyWrapping architecture constrains reference/identity separation, claims, acquired knowledge, terms and performance. Its runtime does not exist as an accepted integration, the shared Encounter contract is not accepted, and field names/schemas/APIs are not frozen. This owner instruction supersedes any contrary interpretation of older proposed-status wording without modifying that evidence. C1's separately accepted bounded non-social EA-C1/0.1.3 proof and finite support requirements are not a general economy implementation; C2.1 B0 remains a reviewed proposal.

**External evidence:** the [Phase 2 research register](../phase-2/RESEARCH_REFERENCES.md) and individual reports retain inspected-source scope, inaccessible material and specialist inferences. They motivate design concerns, not game authority, legal conclusions or calibrated coefficients. This synthesis adds no research or stronger empirical claims.

**Proposed Economy direction:** the selections below are the first unified proposal. **Unresolved owner/Governance choices** are linked to [D01-D11](OWNER_GOVERNANCE_DECISIONS.md); recommendations outside Economy's authority remain recommendations, not installed semantics.

## 2. Minimum authoritative economic state

Within a declared bounded scenario, retain enough supported facts to answer these questions:

1. What relevant money/goods quantity or consequential item exists now, where, and at what supported precision? Which condition distinctions affect the selected use?
2. What property/economic interest, custody/possession/control and separately authorized action are actually supported? Which merely asserted interest is recorded instead?
3. Which consequential economic effects actually occurred, involving which supported resource scope, source/destination or declared boundary? Which genuine occurrence and partial effect does later acknowledgment concern?
4. What selected terms or externally supplied duty basis apply, what becomes due, what qualifying performance occurred, what scope received credit and what remains outstanding?
5. Which operational facts matter now: availability, actual interruption, capacity, inputs, access and supported exclusive allocation?
6. Which activity/resource/time coverage was already represented in aggregate, which explicit activity is excluded, and what consequential exceptions and limits of past detail must persist?
7. What accounts actually exist or were authored/changed/destroyed, and through which supported paths did particular actors acquire their contents?

This is a semantic sufficiency list, not a storage layout or demand for a new subsystem. Existing Encounter and accepted KeyWrapping concepts may supply parts; future reconciliation must establish any integration. Record assertions do not answer questions 1-6 by themselves. Actor acquisition remains under the accepted knowledge architecture, not a new Economy knowledge database.

Derived views include saleable stock, available funds after supported restrictions, remaining duty scope, scoped receipts/payments, operating cash surplus and proposed distributions. They should not become independent balances that can disagree with the supporting facts. A reported stock count, claimed profit or alleged debt is F5, even if internally consistent. Statutory liability, injury resolution, belief, suspicion and trust are not new Economy state.

## 3. Money, cash and banks

For the minimum bounded v0.1 design, select one fictional denomination with a declared indivisible counting unit, ordinary nonnegative holdings and no lending, overdrafts, FX or money-creation model. These are limits of the proposed first slice, not claims about all future money.

An unpaid duty may remain outstanding when its payer has no funds. Nonnegative holdings do not erase debt, guarantee solvency or imply that every due payment succeeds.

Cash is a physical quantity in a wallet, till, safe or supported transit custody. Bank money is a credited account position recognized in the selected world facts. A bank statement is an attributed account about that position. Cash and bank positions support common value-movement reasoning but retain different access, custody and payment-channel meanings. No identity for every banknote is required.

Depositing or withdrawing changes form/location and associated positions; it is not income. The same value cannot remain spendable in both forms. Cash handed to a clerk or courier for deposit is cash in that custody, not already credited bank money or wages. Actual cash receipt and actual account credit can be separate supported effects. If a case permits a delay or failure, the selected terms must state the resulting claim/duty; the narrative cannot silently restore departed cash or invent credit. Exact banking settlement, ordering and retry guarantees are deferred to D01/D10, not supplied as a protocol here.

Actual funding constrains purchases, payroll, restitution and payouts. A declared outside payer or initial endowment may supply funds at a stated simulation boundary; a ledger correction never does. Everyday and shadow purchases and wages can use cash; taxes, insurance and major purchasing can use bank channels as the owner direction envisages. Particular channel requirements must be authored, rather than inferred from the label legitimate or criminal.

## 4. Goods, items, inventory and relationships

A **product/goods kind** describes substitutable properties relevant to trade/use. A **pool** represents a supported quantity of that kind with relevant location, condition and origin distinctions. A **consequential item** has supported individual continuity when a case requires it. Increasing detail may distinguish a unit prospectively; it cannot invent an earlier serial number, buyer, witness or exact route. Mixing/splitting pools must preserve consequential distinctions already retained, without requiring a history per bottle or crumb.

Inventory is a selection/view of actual holdings and supported relationships. Physical stock, intended allocation, exclusive allocation, recorded count and expected replenishment are different questions. A promise does not make goods present or exclusively available. A unit in the Player's custody is excluded from shelf availability for anonymous sale even before payment/title change. A counted-but-missing unit is not a second physical unit. Condition, spoilage, consumption, damage and selected transformation require supported causes at the chosen coarse precision, not a molecular accounting model.

Maintain orthogonal relations:

| Relation | Economic question | Does not itself imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property/economic interest | Whose supported entitlement or participation is this? | Current possession, operational authority, knowledge or legal classification |
| Custody/possession | Who physically holds or is responsible for the supported holding? | Ownership, permission to spend/sell, or paid wages |
| Practical control | Who can presently operate/access it? | Legitimate authority or successful action |
| Scoped grant/authorization | Who may do what, for which resource/action scope? | Unlimited management, group assent or general access |

Possession and custody can differ in cases where entrusted responsibility and immediate holding differ. Keep that distinction when consequential; do not require redundant bookkeeping where the case supports only one coarse relationship. Property/title-transfer timing, standing and revocation remain D02. A commercial fixture must supply its basis explicitly rather than adopt a universal payment-implies-title or taking-implies-theft rule. A business share can justify a defined economic participation without granting management, till withdrawals, faction control or collective knowledge.

## 5. Transactions, transfers, payment and fulfillment

A **transaction** is a related commercial episode of terms and effects. It does not promise atomic execution or automatic rollback. F2 distinguishes actual money movement, goods/custody movement, supplied interest change, actual service and consumption. These can occur at different times or partially.

A **transfer** is a supported movement/change of the relevant holding or interest. A **payment** is actual monetary performance for a stated payable purpose. Actual movement, applicability to a duty and credit toward fulfillment are separately supported. Gifts, entrusted cash movement, payroll funding and bank conversion are not automatically payment of a purchase or wage duty.

An **obligation** identifies supplied required performance and its basis. An agreement, the occurrence making something due, actual performance and credited fulfillment remain distinct. A receipt can claim payment without payment; payment can occur without a receipt; delivery can occur without payment. Neither failure erases completed effects. Refund, return, recovery and compensation are new supported effects with their own basis.

Select no duplicate effect for acknowledgment of the same occurrence, and no duplicate credit for the same required performance scope. Genuine later wages or a second real purchase remain possible. Across different duties, this proposal recommends explicit disjoint allocation in the first fixture; general cross-duty credit, priority, remedies and cancellation require D01. Overlapping promises can exist as outstanding claims; they cannot both be represented as exclusive availability of the same stock. The default first fixture uses explicit allocation, with unallocated demand remaining unmet rather than silently fulfilled.

## 6. Business operations, wages and results

An F4 operation combines offerings, goods/services, resource inputs, staff/capabilities, hours, actual availability, storage/service capacity, authorized actions and selected costs/duties. Grocery, clothing shop, venue, employer, warehouse, rental home, fence and mixed business reuse this structure. Content chooses product mix, quality, prices, capacity and access. No general market-clearing, national supply chain, legal entity, faction or management simulation is required.

Work performed, eligibility under supplied wage terms, due wages, payroll funding, actual worker receipt and payroll descriptions remain separate. Cash withdrawn into payroll custody has not paid anyone. Payroll is repeated/batched F3 duties and F2 payments, not another money system. Absence alone does not distinguish sham employment from paid leave/standby; actual payment remains real even where an attendance record lies.

Select a **scoped cash-flow operating result** as the first comparison basis: included actual operating receipts less included actual operating payments during a declared interval. Call it cash surplus/shortfall unless its basis is displayed. Do not equate it with accounting profit, tax profit, all available wealth or distributable return. Count supplier/restock payment once; do not charge the same outflow again as inventory cost. Owner injections, transfers among own cash/bank holdings, purchase of another owner's interest and borrowed funds are not sales revenue. Insurance settlements are separately identified receipts, not fabricated sales.

An approved distribution basis, actual available funds and actual payout remain separate. A percentage interest does not automatically withdraw that percentage of gross revenue or settle unpaid wages/rent. For the minimum fixture recommend distribution only from an explicitly authorized amount after the fixture's selected obligations/reserves, using fully specified terms. General creditor priority and corporate solvency rules are D01/D02. Derived results do not create cash.

Services represent actual performance/capacity/access without compulsory merchandise. Paying venue admission buys a supported access opportunity, not friendship, information, consent or a successful social encounter. Service-specific outcomes, gambling outcomes/stakes and coercion/consent authority remain D09.

## 7. Accounts, off-books abstraction, tax, insurance and fraud

Official books, private books, receipts, manifests, stock counts, attendance logs, insurance claims and tax reports use F5. Their creation/content history may be authoritative world facts while their assertions about sales, work, ownership, loss or payment are false. Consistency, official status, secrecy and authentic signature do not certify asserted commercial truth.

An **off-books/secret ledger** is an optional account and access arrangement. It may be an abstract supported account or a physical record if a case needs one. It is neither a mandatory notebook nor privileged hidden truth. Both official and private books may lie differently. Destroying a record may prevent future access to it; it does not erase actual effects, protected history or already acquired fragments, and it does not guarantee another surviving clue.

Tax uses separately supplied game-defined duty/reporting bases. Actual activity, report, assessment and payment are distinct. The cash-flow result selected above is not automatically the tax base. Recommend one explicitly authored fictional basis for a later bounded proof; rate, coverage, due point, authority and response require D01/D04. External research does not install real jurisdictional rules.

Insurance combines selected terms, premiums, actual loss, attributed claim, coverage/payout decision, actual payment and later supported recovery. A payout moves insurer funds; it does not restore lost goods. Recovery moves supported original goods; it does not create replacements or automatically reverse money. Salvage, disclosure and repayment duties require explicit D05 terms. A recovery after an honest loss does not retroactively prove fraud.

Fraud is representable through actual conduct/effects, divergent assertions, supported authority/mental facts where supplied, and a separately authored normative question. A discrepancy alone proves neither intent, crime nor detection. Economy supplies no global guilty flag, automatic auditor, privileged lie detector or suspicion propagation. Correcting an account cannot replenish stock, pay wages or remove causal source.

## 8. Warehouse and delivery flow

Select the local flow **declared outside supply -> warehouse -> transit -> local business -> use or onward movement**. National manufacturing and every anonymous customer lie outside the minimum boundary. A boundary inflow/outflow states the goods/value scope and cause at retained precision; it is not unexplained creation/destruction.

A warehouse is an operation with holdings, capacity and access. An order/expected delivery, payment, allocation, dispatch, transit custody, partial receipt and accepted fulfillment are separable. A manifest does not move goods. If ten units are in transit and three are diverted, their supported new custody and the remaining shipment persist. Later receipt cannot add the original ten as well. Already received portions remain separate from interrupted portions. Combat supplies any violence/damage facts; Economy does not resolve an ambush or retroactively author witnesses.

## 9. Aggregate activity and increased detail

Aggregate background activity is coarse representation of the same F1-F4 economy. It is eligible when anonymous/coarse resources, counterparties and cadence suffice for the selected consequence, while support for consequential effects remains available. A named disputed item, partial consequential payment, interruption or actor-specific observation requires supported explicit detail for that concern. The rest of a business can remain aggregate.

Each represented activity needs declared resource/activity/time coverage and explicit exclusions sufficient for single counting. These are semantic questions, not selected field names. Aggregate sales reduce the same stock and add the same supported receipts as explicit sales. They never maintain parallel money/stock. Aggregate wages cannot duplicate explicit wage payments. A Player-held shelf item is excluded from aggregate availability.

Increasing detail preserves opening positions, already represented changes and consequential exceptions. It partitions or refines supported present facts; it is not another sale, shipment or source of money. Explicit activity can replace part of represented aggregate coverage or be genuinely additional activity outside it. Whether Player trade displaces anonymous demand is a content choice under D08; no-overlap is invariant in either case.

Prospective detail starts from supported current facts. If the past retained only an anonymous sale of some unit, a later dispute cannot invent a named buyer, serial, exact time or witness. Where linkage is supported, retain it; where unsupported, preserve unresolved association. New testimony is an account, not a truth-making reconstruction. Known consequential history cannot be deliberately forgotten and relabeled unknown to make promotion cheap. Ordering, identity allocation and runtime promotion mechanics remain D03/D10.

## 10. Time, needs, housing and local opportunity

Use Encounter logical time, employer-specific schedules and local authored activity windows. Expected opening, actual staffing/availability, due time, actual payment, receipt of communication and discovery remain different anchors. Select a small authored range of local demand conditions constrained by stock, access, funds and capacity; no universal weekend multiplier or macroeconomic model.

Payroll funding -> worker receipt -> later spending is a chain of separate effects. Payday redistributes accessible value, not a second source of it. Recommend staggered employer paydays and differentiated weekend/nightlife opportunities for later fixture content. Closed or interrupted activity does not earn its hypothetical normal receipts; unrelated rent or custody can still matter. Skipped time cannot replay covered periods or recover every missed opportunity. Waiting can legitimately permit genuinely new supported earnings and costs. Exact offscreen earnings, opportunity displacement, cadence and catch-up policy are D08 choices.

Household pressure uses supplied Encounter condition consequences and economic access. Select simple Health/Sleep/Hunger/Hygiene concerns, with goods/services and coarse condition distinctions as needed; do not invent a second authoritative health or injury model. Buying food is not consuming it; renting a bed is not sleeping. Food quality/spoilage, clothing tiers, hygiene and upkeep are content, not additional primitives. Substance effects remain abstract future-burden direction without medical rates or implementation.

Housing is access to a service bundle such as sleep, hygiene and storage under selected terms, plus recurring rent/upkeep duties. Occupancy, access, payment and property interest differ; eviction/remedies are not inferred automatically. Preserve the owner's contextual clothes/hygiene threshold for richer engagement and the existing exceptions for basic/emergency transactions, simple instructions and refusal. This is not a universal social-worth score or automatic trust purchase.

Recommend at least one modest basic recovery route and a viable legitimate progression route in the first playable content slice. Owner D06 must choose the recovery floor, failure policy and progression ceiling; this proposal does not install free resources, terminal failure or guaranteed social assent. Work/venues create occasions for social interaction rather than buying relationships.

A phone is capability/access content. The recommended D07 minimum is display of eligible already acquired messages/references, with notes authoring deferred under accepted KW-08. Phone replacement neither resets learned knowledge nor creates new acquisition; unavailable device records depend on their actual storage/access support. Economy cannot amend KeyWrapping reference-authoring rules through an inventory feature.

## 11. Shadow reuse, fences and laundering

Legitimate and shadow activity use the same money, goods, holdings/interests, terms/performance, operations and fallible accounts. Diversion, hidden interest, mixed business, no-show payroll and service vice are compositions/content. An activity label does not change stock physics, grant authority, establish intent or inform police. People are not inventory goods; service performance does not establish consent.

A fence is an ordinary constrained reseller, with product fit, finite liquidity, storage/service capacity, access and supported downstream opportunity. It can refuse or buy partially. Unsold goods remain; theft need not start with a fence order. No universal buyer, guaranteed liquidation, constant resale percentage or mandatory criminal outlet engine is selected.

Laundering is representable as **supported actual source and movement plus a different asserted source/disclosure**. Depositing, mixing, reselling or describing funds as revenue does not erase their retained provenance, settle legality or guarantee institutional acceptance. Commingling can preserve coarse causal source quantities without tracking every note; do not claim exact ancestry after mixing beyond retained support.

Real business capacity constrains real trade, not how much a false account can assert. A small business can claim huge revenue; this remains a fallible claim, with no automatic truth validator or police notification. Recommend D11 option A for the minimum substrate: ordinary supported access/spending and concealment only, no special laundering reward. Option B later may let a particular acquired report influence a specified counterparty decision under separately authorized content. The owner must select that intended benefit; anticipated future enforcement cannot be counted as a current balancing cost. A generic clean-money conversion is rejected.

## 12. Interfaces and bounded history

| Boundary | Economy can propose/supply conceptually | Authority retained elsewhere |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Encounter/Core | Economic fact meanings, support needs and proposed bounded cases | Admitted identity/effects, validation, time, ordering, history and consequences; no installed API or adapter |
| Accepted KeyWrapping architecture | Exact commercial scopes; actuality/accounts/acquisition separation; performance and reference constraints | Existing semantic authority; runtime/shared contract and representations still open |
| Laws/Regulation | Supported action/resource/authority facts and separately supplied normative-basis reference; attributed assessments remain accounts | Jurisdiction, classification, mental/consent questions, adjudication, remedies and enforcement; no automatic theft/fraud from discrepancies |
| Information Economy | Which record exists, supported access history and eligible acquired inputs | Discovery, acquisition, disclosure, inference and response under accepted knowledge constraints; no omniscient ledger access or new alias joins |
| Combat | Holdings/costs/interruption needs and supported economic aftermath | Violence, injury, damage/coercion resolution; payment or art does not resolve combat or intent |

The first future consumer must be named and bounded before any shared integration proposal (D10). These interface descriptions are requirements, not executable contracts. Unknown/shared matters stay open rather than receive invented runtime semantics.

Retain consequential support over a **finite declared envelope**: duration, activity scale, supported precision, protected causal history, active duties, aggregate coverage/exceptions and same-occurrence/performance support. No unlimited campaign history, retrospective oracle, compaction proof, migration guarantee or storage design is selected. Closing an obligation or summarizing a period does not authorize deleting protected history.

Recommend a bounded fixture preserving the protected causal prefix required by the applicable accepted Core proof. D03 must set the actual envelope and response at its limit. The recommended semantic policy is to decline/stop unsupported continuation before losing required support, rather than silently forget a payout, adverse event or retry identity. No runtime error, storage threshold or failure protocol is manufactured here.

The [minimum proof set](MINIMUM_PROOF_SCENARIOS.md) is proposed for later authorization, with all original fifteen documentary cases explicitly carried forward. Nothing here claims executed conservation, sealing, exploit resistance, performance or fun. **Stop at the owner review gate before Phase 4 or any independent audit.**
