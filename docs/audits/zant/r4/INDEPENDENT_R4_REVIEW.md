**Remaining issue before freezing**

In `B_off_NN_NN`, I read small shelf-like changes in the upper tee outline as cap/torso contour ledges rather than a fully continuous shoulder slope. They appear around anatomical R/image-left body (400,365) and anatomical L/image-right (601,365), visible in `CURRENT_native_5bg_upper_pair.png` and the native pair. Both arm surfaces first have alpha support at body y365.

At alpha >127, the assembled outline spans x405–595 on y364, then x400–601 on y365: outward jumps of 5 and 6 pixels. The preceding two rows also flatten briefly. The measurements locate and corroborate the visible steps; they are not an automatic aesthetic-failure threshold. I recommend **holding the neutral contour freeze until these ledges are resolved or the owner explicitly accepts them as intended cloth contour**. This is a localized native finish issue, not evidence that the arms need wholesale reconstruction, that hidden skin must match a jacket trace, or that the modular architecture fails. Under the current instruction against exposed assembly boundaries, they should not be silently propagated into later states.

The A16 material decision is separate: I found no coverage hole or demonstrated broken waist geometry that independently forces rejection. Nevertheless, the owner should explicitly accept or reject the authored waistband and visible upper-pants blend before treating A16 as an approved neutral surface. A checksum or source-alpha match cannot make that decision.
