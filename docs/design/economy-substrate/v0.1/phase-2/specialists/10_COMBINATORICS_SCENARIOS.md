## Proposed representational and semantic requirements

These are **PROPOSED requirement candidates**, not fields, record families, schema constraints or a selected global model. Labels are artifact-local navigation. They apply equally to permitted, concealed and normatively prohibited activity.

| Candidate | Proposed requirement and basis |
| --- | --- |
| C-C01 Scoped flow | Discuss actual movement, consumption, transformation and declared external sources/sinks separately. State the relevant goods/value boundary; do not assume the entire city is closed. Context section 46; C-01 analogy. |
| C-C02 Actuality versus account | Keep actual physical/value effects distinct from recorded inventory, claims, receipts and reporting. Arithmetic agreement is insufficient causal evidence. KW-03/10; C-03 caution. |
| C-C03 Interest versus custody | Distinguish ownership interest, custody, control and scoped authority; none silently supplies the others. Stage 12 inventory E4; property rules remain shared questions. |
| C-C04 Separate performances | Preserve terms, payment, transfer, delivery and fulfillment as distinguishable, possibly partial occurrences. No implicit atomic sale, rollback or refund. Accepted KW-13 and C07-C12. |
| C-C05 Plural fallible records | Official and off-books accounts can both lie or omit. Recording, correcting or destroying evidence cannot rewrite the underlying effect or teach every actor. KW-03/06. |
| C-C06 Scoped repeat distinction | Distinguish retry of one effect, repeated acknowledgment, fulfillment credit and a genuine second effect. Preserve once-only credit toward the same required scope; cross-duty allocation stays open. KW transaction C09; C-02 caution. |
| C-C07 Aggregate coverage | Identify which coarse activity already accounts for stock/value changes and which explicit exception concerns that same scope. Increased detail must not add the same economic effect again. Context section 46; KW-15. |
| C-C08 Supported continuity | Preserve consequential provenance at retained precision. Later significance cannot invent exact prior buyers, units, times or witnesses. Missing detail remains unavailable; finite retention is a constraint. KW-05/07/15; C1 qualifications. |
| C-C09 Audience separation | Identity, employment, ownership, location and record existence confer no automatic acquisition or inference. Evaluate actor-facing claims using eligible acquired information. KW-02/04/06. |
| C-C10 Conjunction review | Examine quantity/capacity, funding, partial performance, coverage and retention together. Individually coherent proposals need not compose under ordering, interruption or repetition. C-04 caution; no proof claimed. |
| C-C11 Classification boundary | Separate economic effects and asserted explanations from normative classification, discovery, enforcement and Combat outcomes. Phase 1 P1-09/10/11. |
