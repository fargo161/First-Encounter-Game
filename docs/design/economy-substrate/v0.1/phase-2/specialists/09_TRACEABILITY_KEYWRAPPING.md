## Specialist findings

Three provenance questions remain irreducible: **actual lineage** of admitted economic effects; **asserted lineage** in documents/testimony; and **holder-acquired lineage** available through eligible acquisition. A forged invoice has real creation/delivery history while its asserted purchase may never have happened. “Trace” therefore cannot be one truth-bearing record kind.

Consequential movement needs a causal basis at its retained precision, including actual supported source/destination and quantity or item scope. This does not require the actor to know that basis, every cash note to have identity, or an anonymous sale to contain a named customer. Motive must not be filled from a business classification or an actor's assertion.

The official/off-books distinction concerns attributed descriptions and access, not two parallel economic realities. Destroying evidence changes its availability through supported world consequences; it does not erase the earlier transfer, observations already acquired, or protected history. Future Information Economy determines discovery and interpretation. Laws determines normative classification under separately accepted rules; Combat resolves relevant violent/damage effects; Factions cannot acquire collective knowledge or assent through payroll membership.

## Documentary cases for later challenge

These are conceptual expectations, not executed simulations, tests or audit passes.

1. **Payment, document and reception.** A validated payment moves value. A commercial receipt may falsely say “paid”; its delivery creates a communication receipt only for what the recipient actually receives. Neither document nor reception manufactures payment, comprehension, belief or assent. Conversely, actual payment can occur without a commercial receipt. E-C01–03; KW-03/06/10.
2. **Two false books.** An official entry says wages; an off-books entry says a supplier payment; actual movement went elsewhere. Preserve both attributed accounts and actual movement. A fabricated shipment claim needs no actual shipment. Authentic document authorship does not authenticate its allegations. No accountant, partner or police actor learns all three merely through their role. E-C01–03; P1-05/08.
3. **Destroyed evidence.** A notebook is destroyed after a transfer. Its prior creation, destruction, transfer and previously acquired fragments remain historically distinct. Someone who never read it gains no content from destruction; an earlier recipient's acquisition is not undone. Whether any other evidence survives is a supported scenario fact, not a mandatory clue generator. E-C02/03/06.
4. **Alias and payroll.** The Player knows an unnamed worker and separately an account-holder label. Hidden canonical equality cannot join those handles, reveal wages or expose a concealed ownership interest. Employment and cash custody confer neither ownership nor knowledge of payroll. Learning the identity link later does not grant historical payslips, other actors' memories or group authority. E-C03; P1-04/08/11.
5. **Credit and allocation.** A supported payment of 10 toward a 30-unit duty supplies 10 once under the fixture's terms; replaying its commercial receipt supplies nothing further. A second actual payment is separate evidence. Whether one payment also discharges a different duty needs an explicit allocation/overlap policy; the same-scope prohibition does not settle that policy. Information promised alongside payment remains independent performance. E-C04; KeyWrapping C09/C10; P1-03/12.
6. **Aggregate promotion.** Background sales already reduced a stock pool and increased revenue over a retained interval. A later consequential phone cannot create a second sale or acquire an invented exact buyer, serial, minute or witness. Preserve only supported continuity; if the phone cannot be linked to that outflow, leave the link unresolved. Promotion does not teach the Player, manager or police anything. E-C01/05; P1-06.
7. **Projection surfaces.** Hold acquired context and authorized outward effects fixed while changing hidden payroll/aliases. Actor-visible labels, counts, ordering, searches, exports, menus and guessed-reference errors must not reveal the change. Knowing a deal's label grants no terms or hidden-child count. Authorized new dialogue can disclose something deliberately; private diagnostics remain privileged. E-C03; KeyWrapping P07–P15; E-02 analogy.
8. **Finite consequential history.** Long-running payroll, receipts and disputes can exceed C1's finite support. A summary cannot promise exact source reconstruction it discarded, silently erase consequential effects, or turn evidence destruction into history collection. Stop/deferral, retained coarse guarantees and future storage approaches require explicit owner/Core decisions. No indefinite progress or compaction mechanism is selected. E-C06; P1-07.
