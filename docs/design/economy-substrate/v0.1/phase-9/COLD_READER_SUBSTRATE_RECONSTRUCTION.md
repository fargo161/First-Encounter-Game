## Reconstruction

Economy is a proposed specialization of Encounter meaning, with one admitted world/history authority. It is not five engines or five record types. F1 asks which resource positions and separately supported economic relationships exist. F2 describes actual economic effects. F3 supplies exact required scope and qualifies/credits the same F2 occurrence. F4 supplies operating configuration, actual availability and capacity; opportunities derive from those facts. F5 supplies attributed economic account content and its actual artifact/communication context, with actor acquisition separately constrained by accepted KeyWrapping architecture.

A supported position, an expected delivery, a duty to deliver, a transfer, a fulfillment credit and a receipt saying delivered can disagree without multiplying goods or history. Likewise cash, bank credit, a pending-credit claim and a bank statement are different. A schedule is prospective. A report cannot create money, a theft, a qualifying wage or a tax basis. A genuine valid institutional act can change a relation only through supplied standing and exact-scope guards; the candidate does not make all documents causally inert.

Ownership/economic participation, custody responsibility, immediate possession, practical control and permission vary independently when supported. A share grants no automatic management, Factions membership, assent or shared knowledge. Payment, transfer purpose, goods delivery and discharge differ. Duties can be partial, late and disputed simultaneously. Exact same-scope credit is once only; general cross-duty allocation remains open.

Ordinary and shadow activities use the same resource/effect/requirement/operation/account meanings. Drugs can be goods and vice can be service without people becoming inventory. A fence is a constrained reseller. Mixing/depositing source-bearing value cannot cleanse its supported past. Real capacity constrains actual trade, not the amount a false account can claim. No future police response can be assumed as a current balancing cost.

Aggregate activity is coarse support for the same history, with declared resource/activity/time coverage and explicit exclusions. More detail adds no old sale, shipment or wage. Current refinement may identify a supported portion prospectively, but cannot invent an earlier buyer, serial, witness or exact time. Unknown historical precision stays unknown. Finite support must retain required terms, credits, causes and exceptions; summaries, closure, salience reduction and destruction grant no deletion permission. No numerical Economy capacity, safe compaction or indefinite campaign guarantee exists.
