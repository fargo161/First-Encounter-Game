**Severity:** NOTE. **Classification:** primary unresolved owner choice; secondary balance/content policy. **Families:** F1/F2/F3/F4/F5.

**Exact path:** a beneficiary receives a real funded payout, recovers originals and realizes their resale value under a compatible no-repayment/no-salvage-transfer policy. **Required assumptions:** supplied claim standing/coverage and real insurer decision; recovery interest remains with the beneficiary; a willing funded buyer with capacity; any repeat is newly eligible and separately funded. **Exact text:** [Phase 4 decision register:40](../phase-4/RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md#L40): “A policy with no recovery-triggered repayment”. **Documentary reproduction:** 1. Supply actual loss and policy/standing. 2. Transfer insurer funds once. 3. Recover supported originals from their current position. 4. Apply supplied title/salvage/disclosure/repayment terms. 5. If they permit retention, sell originals once to a funded buyer. 6. Attempt another payout by replay: blocked. A genuinely new covered event is separately policy-dependent. Thus payout plus resale is possible; infinite repetition is not established.

**Minimum remediation/constraint:** no candidate repair required. Before claiming this loop is balanced or prohibited, state coverage/repeat limits, recovery rights and the intended incentive; any repayment is a new duty/effect. Do not “fix” it by deleting history or inventing automatic detection.

**Owner/Governance choice?** Existing D02/D04/D05, with owner and relevant shared/normative authority; no new architectural decision.
