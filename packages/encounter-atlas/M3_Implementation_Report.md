## Repair and closure

The first reasonable paperwork request is protected. Serious restriction requires the later received evidence pattern and communicates a viable case-only route and explicit visit-long warning. Verified authority persists. Why, drafts/previews/edits, End/reopen, switching partners, reads and rejected commands cannot consume substantive repair. A vague apology is insufficient; case-only acceptance or supported evidence-backed narrowing repairs. Repair preserves warning/history and is not the physical comparison.

Closure requires unrecovered/open service, previously communicated restriction/alternative and warning, a later qualifying sent meaning without new inspected authorization, and the displayed current notice. Preview uses only public warning/current draft/player-known state. Browser display acknowledgment requires a visible viewport rectangle and styling, bound to preview/draft/revision. This is a technical guard, not disclosure of NPC private concern.

No display receipt permits normal receipt/refusal, leaves service open and logs a technical defect. The missing-notice harness deliberately injects this condition; normal browser closure has no such defect. Qualified closure is a distinct physical service result. It is not refusal, mood, question quota or interface consequence. Completed recovery cannot be undone.
