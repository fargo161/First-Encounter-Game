## Hostile method and principal results

For each attempt, require a continuation that obeys all controlling candidate requirements. A path that gains money only by leaving departed cash spendable, repeating a covered period, treating a report as funds, or making recovery clone originals is a **blocked attempt**, not a demonstrated exploit. A path that uses separately funded real transfers under compatible supplied policy is **permitted and policy-dependent**. Where no terms, outcome, feedback or runtime support exists, the dependent result is **unestablished/unresolved**. An **architecture failure** would be a bad path still allowed after applying the existing guards. None was established in these attacks.

The [exploit matrix](EXPLOIT_MATRIX.md) gives 100 individually identified attacks, plus five required combinations, covering every specified example. The [coverage map](REQUIRED_DOMAIN_COVERAGE.md) preserves all fifteen domains, and the [combination report](PATHOLOGICAL_COMBINATIONS.md) constructs all five requested conjunctions with separate resource and authority reasoning. These are documentary reproductions, not executed runtime/play evidence. Any arithmetic is expressly stipulated, not run as simulation.

The strongest successful advantage is D05's explicit compatible no-repayment policy: genuine loss, funded insurance payout, recovery of the same originals, then funded resale can leave more value with the beneficiary than an uninsured recovery would. Repeated staged cycles need additional claim standing, coverage, repeat eligibility, time/access and funding; none is automatically granted. Conservation does not decide whether the owner wants that incentive. Similarly, a no-show may receive real cash despite a false attendance account. That is representable misconduct or another selected wage basis, not manufactured work or a free source of money.

Funded growth and profitable buy/resell trade remain possible. Fences have finite funds/capacity and may refuse, but finite constraints alone do not prove every shadow route has meaningful opportunity cost or that work stays attractive. A legal business may compound through genuinely later supported operation. The model supplies no universal wealth ceiling, return target, guaranteed recovery floor or intended legitimate progression ceiling. D06/D08/D11 must select those policies before corresponding balance claims.

Repeated entry, time skips, forced elaboration, promotion/demotion and reopening a period cannot reset stock or replay covered activity under the text. Unknown historical detail is not a license to forget known exceptions. These are adequate documentary constraints; implementation of atomic consistency, occurrence identity, coverage and finite support remains absent and unproved.

Economic truth cannot decide guilt, reveal crime, grant shareholder powers, buy trust or force police/accountant knowledge. A false report can influence a named recipient only through separately supplied acquired inputs and decision basis. No automatic secret-aware correction was invented to balance fraud.
