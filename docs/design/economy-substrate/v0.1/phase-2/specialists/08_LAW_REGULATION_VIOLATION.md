## Four lanes to preserve

| Lane | Economic example | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actual economic/physical history | Goods changed custody; wages moved; a record was altered | Encounter owns committed effects and causal continuity. A prohibition does not prove an act was physically prevented. |
| Normative classification | A selected game rule may classify that exact conduct, authority and context | Future Laws/Governance owns the shared rule semantics. A rule breach and an attributed allegation are different. |
| Acquired information/evidence | A clerk saw a fragment; an accountant received a record; an insurer heard a claim | Actual receipt does not establish complete truth, belief, intent or legal proof. |
| Institutional action | A later actor decides whether/how to respond | Detection, investigation, adjudication and punishment require their own authority and causes; none is automatic here. |

## Documentary classification cases

These cases identify missing rules and facts; none is a legal verdict, executed test or independent audit.

| Case | Economic facts to preserve | Classification / information boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shelf item carried, unpaid store exit | Custody, actual payment/nonpayment, relevant permission and transfer context | The owner wants shoplifting situations; exact game theft/intent/entitlement rules remain open. No automatic clerk recognition or response. |
| Coerced transfer or damaged shipment | Supported movement/loss and separately supplied coercion/damage occurrence | Combat supplies violent-act/injury truth; narrow economic classification can reference it without absorbing assault/homicide or inferring intent. |
| Banned customer enters | Actual entry, issued policy and its standing/scope | A private ban is not automatically a legal trespass verdict. Knowledge/recognition, lawful authority and physical ability differ. |
| Regulated sale, drug or vice service | Actual goods/service/payment and relevant authored category/context | No real jurisdiction's blanket illegal status is selected. Licensing, possession, sale and service may ask different questions. |
| Tax/reporting discrepancy | Actual aggregate/explicit activity, official account and submitted statement | A mismatch does not prove dishonesty or a specific offence. Reporting duty, knowledge and intent need supported scope. |
| Insurance claim and later recovery | Loss, claim contents, payout, recovery and separate records | Recovery does not retroactively prove the original loss false; coverage, notice, entitlement and possible duty to repay are open terms/rules. |
| No-show employee paid | Employment/authority basis, actual work/nonwork and actual wage payment | Authorized leave, mistake and dishonest payroll must remain distinguishable. A supervisor label does not settle authority. |
| Illicit proceeds enter a business | Actual source/transfer, business activity and recorded classification | Banking or ledger relabeling cannot erase provenance or certify legality; no detection score or laundering method is supplied. |
