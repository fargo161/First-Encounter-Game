## One bounded working loop

**Fixture:** one goods kind used as meals, one warehouse, one store/employer, one worker/household, one rent recipient, abstract bank positions and one declared outside-consumer boundary. No full city, named anonymous shoppers or national supply chain. Supply exact property/access/wage/rent terms and a finite duration/activity envelope before execution. Numbers are fictional counting units, not prices/rates calibrated from research.

| Step | Proposed actual facts | Expected documentary relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Warehouse has 20 goods; store bank position 100; other included monetary positions zero | Declared initial endowment, no hidden income |
| Procurement | Store pays warehouse 20 by bank for 10 goods | Store bank 80, warehouse bank 20; payment does not itself dispatch goods |
| Delivery | 10 goods depart warehouse, then actually arrive at store under supplied terms | Warehouse 10, store 10; transit counted once, manifest alone insufficient |
| Payroll | Worker performs eligible task; 10 becomes due. Store withdraws 10, then pays worker 10 cash | Store bank 70, payroll cash 0 after payment, worker cash 10; funding is not wage receipt |
| Household purchase/use | Worker pays store 3 and actually consumes one acquired meal | Worker cash 7, store till 3, store goods 9; Encounter supplies any condition consequence |
| Rent | Worker pays rent recipient 5 under supplied basis | Worker cash 2, recipient cash 5; occupancy and remedies not inferred from money alone |
| Coarse commerce | Two additional meals leave store for 6 from declared outside consumers; explicit worker purchase excluded | Store goods 7, till 9; outside inflow 6 and goods outflow 2 supported at coarse precision |
| Comparison | Included sales receipts 9, supplier payment 20 and wage payment 10 | Store holdings 79; scoped operating cash result -21. Withdrawal is not a second wage cost; opening 100 is not sales |
| Accounts | Official/private books assert 6/8 receipts respectively for the same scope | Both differ from actual 9; neither report changes money or informs an actor automatically |

Expected balance questions, **not an executed conservation result**: warehouse 10 + store 7 + consumed 1 + outside goods outflow 2 relate to the opening 20. Included money ends as store 79 + warehouse 20 + worker 2 + rent recipient 5 = opening 100 + declared outside inflow 6. No obligation, record or derived result supplies extra funds. The loop demonstrates intended composition without claiming Core support already exists.
