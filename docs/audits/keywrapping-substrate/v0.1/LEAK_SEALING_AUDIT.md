The adversarial method was manual paper reasoning against the actual proposed fields, causal order, examples and stated guards. For hidden-world substitutions, keep the evaluated actor's acquired history and the selected permitted outward effects equal; change only inaccessible world material. Inspect whether the proposed output would need to change without a new eligible acquisition. Deliberately chosen NPC speech/actions can legitimately differ with private concerns, so this comparison does not require every NPC with a different private history to behave identically.

Separate read-only document checks parsed the central fenced JSON with duplicate-key rejection, resolved its declared local references/revisions and inspected the four actor-output blocks for specified privileged field names. These are syntax/reference/field checks, not an implementation or an automated adversarial harness. Semantic conclusions below come from manual review; no runtime projection was executed.

## Concrete example inspection

The central [JSON_RECORD_FAMILY.md](../../../design/keywrapping-substrate/v0.1/JSON_RECORD_FAMILY.md) contains **10 strict JSON blocks**, **64 privileged records**, **18 external declarations** and **243 local reference occurrences**. Read-only parsing found no duplicate object keys or unresolved/mismatched local target revisions. Four blocks are explicitly actor-safe view examples. Searching those four parsed blocks found no `ref`, `worldSnapshot`, `privateIntent`, `actualSource`, `revision`, `hiddenCount`, `canonicalId` or `truth` field. This limited field inspection cannot prove that other strings, encodings, timing or correlations are safe.

Specific causal and disclosure checks:

- `hide0` and `take1` are author-private occurrences. Player-visible accounts come through `secretReceipt` at 3 and `falseReceipt` at 5; occurrence existence alone grants no Player observation. `rayObservation`, `rayAssessment`, actual-source links and the deliberate misattribution intent remain outside the received-accounts view.
- The false-claim view says Ray supplied the account and asserted eyewitness status, while its assessment is unassessed. It does not expose that the guard actually took the key, a lie flag, Ray's disbelief or the privileged record name `falseAccount`. A changed hidden taker, with unchanged received accounts, requires the same attributed Player output under the model's stated rules.
- The proposal's information topic and satisfaction criterion are disclosed terms; the undisclosed account payload is not an unknown fee/condition. Guard background knows the topic, not the payload. The actual payload delivery is `tellReceipt` at 24, not the earlier OFFER or assent. `tellCredit` at 25 uses delivery evidence without declaring truth or belief. Cancellation cannot erase that received information.
- Both parties receive the chosen full V2 terms and have separate current self-assent evidence. Required understanding is an explicit paper assumption, not a conclusion from receipt. The actual grant follows emission/receipt at coarse tick 19, and has a separately declared authority scope; it does not represent physical entry or general knowledge access.
- The standalone promise uses the explicitly selected beneficiary-delivery rule and creates its duty after `promiseReceipt` at 27. Preparing `promiseSpeech` or creating its reference cannot acquire the promise for the guard early. This fixture rule does not settle global promise policy.
- Hidden promotion at 40 does not insert the shopkeeper into the Player book. The meeting at 50 supports only the role label; the introduction at 60 supports the later asserted name. The historical view retains the role label, and the current source records the self-introduction. Changing a private canonical name while preserving that received introduction must not silently authenticate or change the label.
- The deal/dispute output declares a separate outward-knowledge context, with externally assumed acquisition of both topic/relationship handles. It does not pretend that the main fixture's earlier Player receipts delivered the guard's later allegation. Its two visible edges close a bounded cycle without allegation contents or hidden-neighbor counts.

Partial hearing, multi-hop rumors, false two-person association and guessed-handle failures have explicit prose guards but no executable output implementation. Their matrix entries below are paper/control evaluations; the complete-expression examples do not demonstrate a running partial-receipt mechanism.
