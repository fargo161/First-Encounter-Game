## Sequence and handoffs

1. **Preflight and owner sign-off.** The read-only A–M preflight preceded branch creation and production files. The owner accepted the recommended scope with the Context comparison condition and publication hold. The original configured checkout was unavailable; a full clone in the approved workspace restored repository access. The historical preflight and access-restoration addendum remain intact.
2. **Research/mining.** Roles 2–5 researched their architectural problems and separated donor semantics, Encounter adaptations, rejected assumptions, licensing and gaps. The lead/steward inspected repository authority and source precedence. VerbNet's 329 pinned XML files were inspected outside the checkout; no donor corpus was vendored.
3. **KeyWrapping/schema synthesis.** Roles 6–7 received the completed four mining reports before synthesis. They coordinated the warehouse fixture's source account, counteroffer and partial payment. The lead reconciled the result with the owner source pack and added the integrated specification. Lead review requested explicit candidate action/predicate signatures, distinct normative permission/prohibition and a unilateral promise that can form a duty without an invented agreement. The engineer incorporated those as declared paper policies, leaving global promise rules open.
4. **Independent sealing review, completed.** Role 9 reviewed the integrated snapshot, concrete actor projections and 27 hostile cases. It reported SL-01 (medium): one reference-correction sentence inferred belief from an acquired identity association. The lead corrected R11 to preserve the acquisition/assertion and any separately explicit stance, with no inferred Player belief. Role 9 independently rechecked and closed it before the next audit began. Its report retains original/revised hashes and operational limits; no unresolved critical/high documentary finding was identified within scope.
5. **Independent combinatorial review, completed.** A different fresh reviewer began only after the sealing correction/recheck cycle closed. Role 10 checked all 66 words, all 329 pinned VerbNet XML files, all 66 complete membership inventories, all 65 selected donor evidence rows, the integrated paper fixture and 50 nonsense/ambiguity cases. It reported CS-01 (medium): the Hide signature required an audience absent from the physical hiding fixture. The lead separated physical concealment's optional audience from information withholding's required selected content and identified audience. Role 10 independently rechecked and closed the correction. Role 9 then rechecked the affected knowledge boundaries and recorded no additional finding or regression; SL-01 remained closed. The semantic report records no unresolved critical/high/medium documentary finding.
6. **Delivery checks, completed.** The [verification report](VERIFICATION_REPORT.md) records the existing package checks and their preserved known states, strict parsing of all 12 JSON blocks, local references/links, reviewed snapshot identities, source/vocabulary checks and the exact 24-path change inventory. The two independent audit reports are substantive deliverables, not placeholders. No implementation or gameplay result is inferred.

## Evidence and limits

Research findings are not owner design decisions. Synthesis proposals are not accepted contracts. Independent paper review can identify omissions and inconsistent examples; it cannot execute secrecy enforcement, social behavior, runtime replay or gameplay enjoyment. No automated KeyWrapping harness was created. Actual repository checks and conceptual attack cases are labeled separately.

The final Git package is limited to 23 new Markdown documents in the three versioned KeyWrapping trees and the existing `docs/README.md` index addition. The [integration plan](../../../design/keywrapping-substrate/v0.1/GITHUB_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md) gives exact paths and five proposed commit groups. Publication remains held even if the documentary audits close without unresolved findings.
