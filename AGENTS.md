This is the authorized Stages 0-3 foundation for First Encounter Game.

- Read docs/provenance/source-authority.json and docs/consolidation/NEXT_STAGE_GATE.md before work.
- Until the user explicitly authorizes a later stage, do not import subsystem source, normalize source, refactor, remediate Room Creator, repair ZANT, implement shared contracts, initialize Unity, enable LFS, or delete/archive-clean historical material.
- Later explicit user authorization governs its stated scope; do not reopen settled decisions without a concrete blocker.
- Older Trapstar repositories are non-authoritative. Never fork, merge, import their history, or recover content without a specific justified current need and appropriate authorization.
- Keep builder claims, independent findings, integrity checks, and owner acceptance distinct.
- Preserve original artifacts and source trees. Historical draft labels do not override later explicit owner acceptance.
- Keep large or restricted binary collections outside normal Git history.
- Encounter owns gameplay interaction contracts; preserve independent subsystem boundaries.
- The foundation verifier certifies this pre-import stage only. A later authorized import must update the stage and verification scope deliberately.
