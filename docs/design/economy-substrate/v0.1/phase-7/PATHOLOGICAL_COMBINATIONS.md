## PC01 — Payday + aggregate sales + shoplifting + fencing + insurance claim + owner distribution

2. Aggregate sales move two units outward and six money inward: R96, stock8. That coverage excludes the explicit Player act.
3. Player takes one supported unit: R's shelf availability becomes7. Supplied interest/permission/classification remain distinct; minority participation does not grant taking authority.
4. Fence receives that original unit and pays four: fence money6, Player14, fence stock1. Product fit alone did not compel this purchase; willingness/funds/capacity were stipulated.
5. An actual attributed insurance claim leads to the separately stipulated funded payout of five: R101, insurer45. The claim's truth, coverage rights and actual payment remain distinct. It does not restore a unit.
6. Independently authorize/fund Player's two-unit distribution from the supplied pool: R99, Player16. A percentage/report alone cannot perform this step.
7. Reopen payday, aggregate report, claim and dividend: none may repeat its original effect. New periods/events would require new supported basis/coverage/funds.

**Stipulated arithmetic:** ending money R99 + Player16 + fence6 + insurer45 =166, opening160 plus declared outside inflow6. Ending goods R7 + fence1 + outside outflow2 =10. This checks only the chosen documentary arithmetic. It neither proves an implementation nor calls Player16 profit after all original resource/time/interest costs.

## Emergence conclusion

These compositions demonstrate why conservation is insufficient to prove desired incentives. Compatible policy can reward unearned pay, recovery windfall, cheap resale or passive ownership; other compatible content can create deprivation/debt traps. None supplies automatic infinity, cloning, source cleansing, social authority, discovery or enforcement. The [finding register](FINDING_REGISTER.md) records those limits, and the [verdicts](GAMEPLAY_AND_SYSTEM_VERDICTS.md) withhold unsupported experience claims. **STOP before Phase 8; no fix or cold test was run.**
