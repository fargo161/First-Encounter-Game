# Supplied asset license/status records

The Room Creator asset-library source manifest and DOCUMENTATION/LICENSE_REVIEW.md are preserved in the private external package.

The supplied record lists 327 models, 1043 surface textures and three palettes. Twenty-nine vehicle assets from two car packs retain unverified supplied license status. The pixel-art texture pack restricts raw-pack redistribution/repackaging. CC0 records for other packs remain preserved.

This foundation publishes metadata only. It does not grant redistribution rights, remove warnings, or change any supplied license. Review exact source records before selecting assets for later Git tracking or distribution.
