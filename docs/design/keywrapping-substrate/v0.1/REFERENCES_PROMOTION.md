# References, identity, promotion and acquisition

**Version:** v0.1 · **Date:** 2026-10-22
**Status:** PROPOSED / NON-RUNTIME / NOT AN ACCEPTED SHARED CONTRACT
**Role:** KeyWrapping Architect, after all four mining reports

This is an Encounter design proposal for documentary and paper review. It specifies logical distinctions, guarded changes and examples; it installs no registry, persistence mechanism, runtime module or accepted contract. The [source snapshot](../../../research/keywrapping-substrate/v0.1/SOURCE_SNAPSHOT.md) gives source identities and precedence. The owner's production sign-off governs scope. C2.1 B0 is comparison input only. No choice here modifies accepted contracts, frozen packages, C1, C2.1 B0, Room Creator or ZANT.

## 1. Source-backed direction and proposed model

KW-CONTEXT §§4–12, 17, 20–23 and 26–28 establish earned vocabulary, NPC-anchored significance, sealed meaning and no Player authoring interface. They distinguish a consequential world record from an actor learning it. Stable internal anchors, typed links and retention dimensions are recommended refinements. KW-REVIEW adds label and identity-merge concerns; KW-REPLY's permanent learned-book access is a recommendation awaiting owner selection. The [provenance miner](../../../research/keywrapping-substrate/v0.1/PROVENANCE_DONOR_MINING.md), especially KP-01–KP-14, supplies the information-boundary recommendations carried forward here.

All record organization and guard rules below are **Encounter proposals** unless explicitly described as established direction. They avoid treating the Context's illustrative tiers or JSON as a ratified lifecycle.

| Concept | Proposed responsibility | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World identity | Preserve continuity of a person, object, place or other authoritative referent inside a declared world/artifact scope | Does not give any actor a name, recognition or knowledge of another alias |
| Hard Keyword | Address a recognized game entity, resource, kind or place with selected supported meanings | Stable identity does not freeze custody, condition, ownership or permissions |
| Wrapper | Make a typed social/historical subject addressable: person, proposal, agreement, promise, event account, claim, dispute, plan or relationship | Addressability does not certify truth, permanence, knowledge or world capability |
| Subject of an account | Distinguish an actual occurrence from an alleged, hypothetical, fictional or unresolved subject | An account need not have a matching canonical occurrence |
| Audience handle | Let a particular audience refer to an acquired subject within an authorized view | Never exposes a canonical joining key or grants recursive access |
| Audience label | Present a label supported by that audience's acquisitions, with attribution when needed | A title is information and can itself contain an allegation |
| Identity association | Record that one actor learned, inferred or was told that two acquired handles identify the same subject | Holder-local assertion is not a global person merge or disclosure of all linked history |
| Acquisition | Record an actor's legitimate receipt, observation, authorized background or bounded inference of particular information/reference | Receipt is not belief, endorsement, assent or access to every related record |
| Promotion | Record a decision to preserve or elaborate continuity/significance because a qualifying encounter or consequence needs it | Increasing simulation attention does not disclose facts or improve combat power |
| Salience and availability | Describe current processing/presentation priority and separately what a holder may use or inspect | Archiving does not undo events; loss of emphasis does not decide forgetting |

This vocabulary aligns with [PERSPECTIVE_PROVENANCE.md](PERSPECTIVE_PROVENANCE.md) and the illustrative [JSON record family](JSON_RECORD_FAMILY.md). Logical roles do not require separate files, tables, services or separate wrapper objects for every sentence.

## 2. Identity and reference qualification

**Proposed rule R01 — privileged identity.** A canonical reference carries producer, exact artifact/world scope, local kind and local identity; an exact revision is supplied where historical meaning depends on it. Its scope prevents accidental joins across separate runs or fixtures. Existing native IDs and their version/hash scopes remain native. An illustrative Encounter reference to a Room Creator point or ZANT asset grants neither subsystem mutation authority nor an installed binding. Basis: repository boundary matrix; KW-CONTEXT §§4.1–4.4, 21; KP-01/KP-08.

**R02 — actor handle.** An actor-facing handle is opaque within its audience/view scope. An actor projection must not carry the corresponding privileged reference, canonical ID fragments, stable hashes, globally deterministic pseudonyms or reverse-lookup links that reveal undiscovered equality. Two handles may resolve internally to one person while remaining unconnected in the Player's view. Cross-audience handle equality is not promised. Encoding, rotation and long-term handle stability remain OPEN. Basis: KW-REVIEW, “Design for the merge”; KP-05/KP-06 and alias analysis.

**R03 — typed links.** A wrapper's subject kind determines the slots it can fill. An event wrapper cannot be given as a physical key; a claim about ownership is not an ownership grant. Links identify a relation and its scope rather than copying all nested content. Traversal follows the per-edge projection and work bounds in the perspective model. No default expansion, automatic inverse relation or global logical closure follows a reference. Basis: KW-CONTEXT §§4.2, 17, 20; action dictionary shared rules; KP-02/KP-09.

**R04 — wrapper truth neutrality.** A false murder report can become an addressable claim and alleged-event subject because its utterance mattered. It does not create an actual murder occurrence. A later discovery can link an account to a verified occurrence through a new, qualified assertion; it does not retroactively turn all earlier hearsay into observation. If an actor misidentifies two different people, retain that actor's association as mistaken/contested rather than merging the world identities. Basis: KW-CONTEXT §19; KP-03/KP-04 and phantom-allegation control.

## 3. Promotion is independent of learning

The established eligibility direction is an encounter creating or revealing something the game may need to refer back to (KW-CONTEXT §§5–8). An NPC must participate in or be materially affected by the qualifying Player encounter; the NPC need not hear, identify the Player, consent or survive. A name exchange, refused offer, accusation, theft or death can therefore create continuity. Neither NPC acceptance nor a conscious witness is a universal prerequisite.

**R05 — keep four questions separate.** For each consequential case, the documentary model asks:

1. What actual occurrence or communication/evidence must remain causally traceable?
2. Is an addressable wrapper subject needed now or available as a candidate for later use?
3. What continuity/elaboration or active attention does that subject currently warrant?
4. What did this particular actor acquire, and which part may enter that actor's usable vocabulary now?

No answer automatically supplies the other three. These are conceptual questions, not mandatory sequential states or a fixed promotion enum. Basis: KW-CONTEXT §§6, 9–12; KP-03/KP-13.

**R06 — recorded significance basis.** A proposed promotion decision retains the triggering encounter/consequence, affected identity/subject, reason, effective point and selected policy revision in privileged evidence. It can point to an obligation needing continuity or an irreversible event needing enduring causal identity. That bookkeeping does not become an automatic Player notification. No fixed five-tier progression, numeric significance threshold, decay curve or allocation budget is selected. Basis: KW-CONTEXT §§5–6, 9–11; provenance miner's promotion/time analysis; social donor report's rejection of universal importance scores.

**R07 — controlled accumulation.** Repeating an identical request or pleasantry need not create another enduring article. Distinct communications retain their applicable history; wrapper reuse or later promotion can group a meaningful continuing subject without collapsing the communications into one event. The deduplication/grouping policy must distinguish semantic subject reuse from replay of the same historical record. Exact thresholds are OPEN. Basis: KW-CONTEXT §§6–7, 17; KP-10/KP-11.

## 4. Acquisition, labels and identity discovery

**R08 — evidence-bound acquisition.** Acquiring a handle or new detail needs a holder, acquired scope, actual learning point, acquisition mode and eligible evidence/background basis. Observation grants only perceived aspects; testimony grants an attributed account; inference retains its premises and uncertainty. Authored starting knowledge is a scenario option recommended by KW-REVIEW, not a default omniscient prefill or new owner-approved starting book. Its contents and their Player causal/background justification require scenario selection. Basis: KW-CONTEXT §§8, 12, 20, 23, 25; KP-03/KP-10.

**R09 — label ownership.** Use labels based on the holder's acquired information: “the shopkeeper,” “the shooting at the hotel,” or “what Ray calls Marcus's betrayal.” The last label retains attribution and does not adopt guilt as neutral narration. A heard name can be an asserted name without authenticating legal identity. No official globally disclosed wrapper title is required. Label changes preserve original utterance wording and historical views; current explanatory labels are separately qualified. Basis: KW-CONTEXT §§4.3–4.5, 17; KW-REVIEW §5; KP-05/KP-10.

**R10 — acquired equivalence.** When the Player learns “Eddie is the shopkeeper,” append an identity-association acquisition joining the already acquired handles for the current authorized view. Preserve its basis and whether it is testimony, inference or an authoritative identification available to the Player. Connect only the information the Player already held or separately acquires. Do not import Eddie's sealed debt, aliases, relatives or old receipts. Earlier views keep the earlier unconnected labels. Another actor learns nothing merely because the Player learned the link. Basis: KW-CONTEXT §§4.3–4.4, 10; KW-REVIEW merge recommendation; KP-02/KP-05/KP-10.

**R11 — correction without historical rewrite.** A later correction can retract or qualify a holder's acquired or asserted association and change current presentation. Preserve the earlier acquisition/source assertion and any separately, explicitly modeled stance; acquiring or repeating an association does not establish that the holder believed it. In particular, do not infer or record an unexpressed Player belief. Correcting a label is not permission to merge/split authoritative world identities or erase consequences already caused by use of the mistaken association. Canonical identity correction, if ever necessary, requires its own scoped authority/integrity design outside this proposal. Basis: KP-04/KP-10/KP-12; Encounter proposal to preserve the baseline's truth/interpretation split.

The Player has no private keyword editor, scratchpad, “wrap” button or unexpressed-thought record (established KW-CONTEXT §7). Expressing a claim using legitimately acquired subjects can produce a new communicative subject through an NPC encounter. It does not permit arbitrary guessed secret IDs or hidden people to be registered as acquired vocabulary. NPC private plans or hypotheses may exist without becoming public wrappers.

## 5. Retention, quieting and reactivation

| Dimension | Proposed minimum preserved meaning | Deliberately unresolved policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical retention | Necessary identity, effect and causal/source evidence survive while needed for correct continuation; major irreversible consequences keep enduring anchors | Exact archive/compaction representation and minor-event retention limits |
| Active salience | Current processing, elaboration and book emphasis may decrease without changing the past or undoing inventory/effects | Thresholds, decay timing, reactivation triggers and resource budgets |
| Player availability | Only legitimately acquired handles/details are eligible; a quiet section can reduce clutter independently of history | Whether every learned entry remains permanently usable; explicit forgetting events and availability rules |
| NPC memory | Current accessible recollection is separate from what once occurred or was received | Forgetting, uncertainty growth and memory costs; no universal decay model |

**R12 — bounded quieting.** Closing an obligation or ending active attention can reduce salience without deleting the occurrence, reference identity, original terms or necessary evidence. Reactivation points to the retained cause and new relevance; it is not a second occurrence or a newly fabricated past. Compaction may not alter accepted terms, irreversible consequences or retained causal facts needed for later continuity. A concrete compaction policy needs separate proof of those invariants; this pass chooses none. Basis: KW-CONTEXT §§9–11; KP-10/KP-13.

KW-REPLY recommends that no legitimately learned Player keyword disappear through housekeeping, with explicit story forgetting as its exception. This package preserves that recommendation as **OPEN**, rather than declaring it adopted. A bookkeeping/archive transition cannot quietly settle the choice. Retention and salience fields in examples describe different questions and are not a disguised permanent-book decision.

## 6. Scoped change guards

These documentary guards are proposed review criteria, not executable transitions.

| Proposed change | Required basis and scope | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish wrapper/candidate | Typed subject and qualifying encounter/consequence/source account | A title alone establishes an actual event or creates Player knowledge |
| Promote continuity | Recorded significance basis and selected policy; stable identity if needed | Proper name, relatives' knowledge or new abilities appear automatically |
| Add holder acquisition | Actual received/observed content, allowed inference or selected authored background; exact holder and point | Intended listener, co-location, wrapper recognition or global graph membership proves receipt |
| Relabel current entry | Holder-acquired label content and attribution | A hidden culprit/cause, unlearned name or objective guilt appears in a title |
| Join holder handles | An acquired identity association with source/status | Shared canonical ID silently joins aliases or imports private histories |
| Correct an association | New holder-eligible evidence and a preserved prior account | Another actor's identities/knowledge silently change |
| Quiet/archive | Retention obligations examined separately from salience | Inventory, death, debt or receipt is undone; learned-book policy silently frozen |
| Reactivate | Retained causal basis plus new relevance | Invented past evidence or duplicate original effects |
| Generate later consequence | Existing facts/relationships and an explicit bounded scenario mechanism | Family or investigators magically know the killer, or a hidden consequence appears in the Player book |

## 7. Paper scenarios for review

These are proposed exercises; no gameplay or software test result is claimed.

| Scenario | Required conceptual result |
| --- | --- |
| Name introduction | Shopkeeper says “I'm Eddie” in an eligible encounter. Player gains the received name/association, not inventory, debt or private opinion. Record whether the claim is authenticated or simply accepted by the Player. |
| Minor theft | An unnamed shopkeeper loses $50 through the Player's act. Preserve the inventory effect, affected identity and causal trace. Attention can fade if no selected follow-up warrants it. Do not reveal a proper name, assign family knowledge, undo the loss or decide permanent Player-book availability. |
| Killing an unnamed shopkeeper | Preserve the victim identity and irreversible death as enduring consequences. Player may know only the observed person/act; nobody else automatically knows the culprit. Later disappearance discovery, testimony or investigation can create separate private concerns and eventual acquired references. |
| Same person, unconnected handles | The Player knows “the shopkeeper” and “Eddie” through different encounters. Opaque handles reveal no canonical equality. An eligible later reveal associates them only at its actual learning point. Sealed debts and the historical view remain unchanged. |
| False re-identification | Ray identifies an innocent person as the shopkeeper. The Player can hold Ray's sourced claim and be mistaken. Do not merge world identities; a later correction preserves the mistaken report and its consequences. |
| Fabricated incident | A repeated false report earns a claim/dispute reference. It has real communication history and an alleged subject, with no fabricated canonical occurrence. |
| Routine repetition | Several identical requests are distinct utterances when actually spoken; they need not create several enduring named wrappers. Retry/reload of the same receipt is not another utterance or acquisition. |
| Later family discovery | A relative discovers absence/death through a selected source path. Their concern can become relevant, but they receive neither killer identity nor the Player's name without evidence. Player acquisition waits for a later eligible encounter/discovery. |

The theft and killing scenes are baseline world-history fixtures, not registration of Steal/Kill/Attack/Hurt as selectable actions. Those four words remain candidate-only in the [66-word dictionary](../../../research/keywrapping-substrate/v0.1/ACTION_PREDICATE_MAPPING.md). A composed utterance needing an unavailable sense must expose that vocabulary gap in paper review.
