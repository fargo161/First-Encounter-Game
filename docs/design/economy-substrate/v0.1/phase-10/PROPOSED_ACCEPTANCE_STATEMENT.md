# Proposed Economy Substrate v0.1 acceptance statement

**Status:** DRAFT FOR OWNER ADOPTION — NOT AN ACCEPTANCE RECORD  
**Date:** 2026-10-23, America/New_York

The exact bounded statement below would apply only if the owner explicitly adopts it. Choice B in the [decision memo](OWNER_ACCEPTANCE_DECISION_MEMO.md) would require the owner's actual conditions to be recorded; this draft supplies none. The scope table and non-acceptance list define the referenced coverage and exclusions. No prior audit or this packaging phase adopts the statement on the owner's behalf.

## Proposed statement

> I accept Economy Substrate v0.1 as the bounded architectural/design baseline for future separately scoped Economy work, as defined by the unchanged Phase 4 reconciled candidate, its five-family structure, mandatory semantic separations and invariants, cross-system boundaries and explicit residual decisions. Phase 10 is a review summary of that baseline and does not amend its architecture.
>
> This design acceptance covers F1 — resource positions and economic relations; F2 — actual economic effects; F3 — required performance and fulfillment; F4 — operating conditions and capacity; and F5 — attributed economic accounts. These are semantic responsibilities within Encounter authority, not five independent engines, stores or runtime classes. Required distinctions inside them must survive future representation choices.
>
> I accept the legitimate/shadow reuse principle, aggregate/explicit single-history continuity, supported causal provenance/traceability, and intentionally bounded, non-microscopic simulation philosophy. Goods, value, duties and credited performances require supported causes and retained consequential continuity at declared precision. Every background transaction or banknote need not be instantiated. Increased detail may not replay covered activity or invent unsupported earlier identities, routes, witnesses or events. Finite history does not authorize forgetting required support to repeat an effect, and does not promise unlimited history or safe compaction.
>
> Actual economic effects, property/economic interests, custody, possession, practical control, authority, exact duties, due state, qualifying performance, payment, goods transfer and fulfillment credit remain separable. F3 qualifies the same F2 occurrence; it does not perform it again. Commercial result, available money, an authorized distributable pool and actual payout remain different. Reports, official accounts, secret accounts and claims may disagree with actuality; they do not create money, goods or alleged events. A genuinely valid current institutional act still requires the actual owning authority's standing and exact-scope conditions.
>
> Encounter remains authoritative for admitted identity, gameplay state/effects, time and protected history. Accepted KeyWrapping reference, claim, acquisition, authority, exact-term and performance constraints continue to govern. Economic provenance and account availability do not automatically become actor knowledge. Economy supplies no independent truth/history or knowledge/reference system and does not infer guilt, enforcement, violence, injury, consent, Faction standing, collective knowledge or institutional response.
>
> The intended coverage below accepts a common architectural vocabulary for representing those activities, not final content, formulas, rates, balance or experience. All D01–D11 residual choices remain explicit and open unless I separately specify a decision. Recommended content defaults and fixture assumptions are not adopted as general policy by this statement. A dependent future claim must supply its actual basis and authority rather than invent an answer.
>
> This acceptance does not accept or authorize any item in the explicit non-acceptance list below. In particular it is not Economy runtime acceptance, shared executable Encounter integration, implementation permission, full integration, gameplay verification or permission to merge or publish. It does not modify accepted Core or KeyWrapping material, ratify an unresolved shared contract, or change other subsystem acceptance/HOLD/failure states. KeyWrapping's existing architectural acceptance remains distinct from its absent/unaccepted runtime and unaccepted shared integration; representations remain unfrozen.
>
> Any implementation, specific policy selection, integration proof, Laws/Combat/Information/Factions work, Unity/Stage 13 activity or Git/publication action requires a later explicit work order. Design acceptance alone starts none of them.

## Intended scope of design acceptance

The [candidate](../phase-4/ECONOMY_SUBSTRATE_V0_1_RECONCILED.md), especially §§2–10, and [boundary coverage](../phase-4/CROSS_SYSTEM_BOUNDARIES.md) supply this scope. No additional activity engine or outcome authority is introduced.

| Intended concern | What the architectural baseline covers | Content/authority left open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player needs / household economy | Food, sleep, hygiene, clothes, medicine and phone access through goods/services, access/use and supplied condition consequences | Final needs rates, recovery/progression/failure, medical/social outcomes; no second health/social truth or Player scratchpad |
| Housing / rent / upkeep | Scoped tenancy/access/storage/services, recurring supplied duties, upkeep costs and actual performance | Terms, accrual basis, due/priority/remedies and recovery guarantees; tenancy is not ownership |
| Stores and restaurants | Stock, use/transformation, offerings, service/work capacity, payment, delivery and attributed accounts | Prices, recipes, service outcomes, availability/demand content and recognition rules |
| Bars and clubs | The same operations, stock/services, paid access, work and accounts | Admission does not buy trust, relationships, consent or acquired information |
| Warehouses and deliveries | Stock, order/payment/allocation, dispatch, transit, partial receipt, diversion and credit over the same effects | Title, exact logistics, violent outcomes, shared ordering and promotion mechanism |
| Money / cash / bank positions | Supported cash holdings versus credited bank positions, pending claims, separately supported conversion and statements | No reserve/money-creation/lending/FX model; exact channels, settlement and failure guarantees open |
| Inventory and goods | Product kind, supported pool, consequential item continuity, split/merge/use/spoilage/transformation and supported origin | No microscopic inventory requirement or invented per-unit past precision |
| Employment / wages / payroll | Work/eligibility/basis/due/funding/pay/credit and attendance accounts remain distinct | Eligibility, priority, exact payroll cadence and remedies |
| Payday and local economic rhythms | Prospective local schedules/conditions using Encounter time and single-counted activity | Weekend/payday multipliers, offscreen extent, demand displacement and skipped-time opportunities |
| Business ownership and distributions | Economic participation versus management grants; stated result basis, authorized pool, available funds and actual payout | Title/authority, profit recognition/distribution basis, passive-growth tuning |
| Official and off-books accounts | Plural fallible attributed records with separately supported access | Optional carrier/interface, disclosure and recipient response; no privileged secret truth |
| Tax and insurance representation | Facts, reports, supplied rules/assessment/duty/pay; policy/loss/claim/coverage/payout/recovered originals | Tax formulas, coverage, salvage/disclosure/repayment policy and enforcement |
| Fraud representation | Actual conduct and false assertions alongside separately supplied authority/mental/normative facts | Discrepancy alone proves no intent, guilt, discovery or punishment |
| Drugs / vice | Ordinary goods or service, inputs/capacity, movement, payment and attributed accounts | Classification, participation/consent and specialized condition/violent outcomes; people are not goods |
| Fencing / organized theft | Constrained ordinary resale and admitted diversion/transport/distribution aftermath | No universal buyer/funds/demand, crime planner, group mind or violence resolution |
| Laundering / mixed businesses | Ordinary source-bearing positions/effects/duties/accounts and different asserted origins/disclosure | No clean-money state or source erasure; intended recipient benefit, acceptance, detection and current costs remain open |

| Engine and integration | Unity work; Stage 13; a shared executable contract or installed cross-system adapter |

The audits and cold verification support documentary coherence at their stated scope. They do not remove an exclusion. **Await the owner's explicit decision; no acceptance or next work is recorded here.**
