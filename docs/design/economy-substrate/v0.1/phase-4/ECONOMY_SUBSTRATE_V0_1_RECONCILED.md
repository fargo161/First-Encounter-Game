## 2. Minimum authoritative economic support

For a declared finite scenario, the candidate requires supported answers to seven questions, at declared precision:

1. What relevant money/goods positions or specific items exist, with which meaningful condition and location/account scope?
2. Which interests, custody/possession, practical control and applicable authorizations are supported independently of assertions about them?
3. Which actual economic occurrences/effect scopes happened, with which supported source/destination/cause or declared outside boundary?
4. Which exact supplied duties/requirements apply, what is activated/due, which actual occurrences qualify, what scope has received credit and what remains?
5. Which operational facts affect activity: actual availability, selected capacity, inputs, access and consequential interruptions?
6. What aggregate resource/activity/time coverage is already represented, what is excluded explicitly, and which consequential exceptions and limits of historical detail remain?
7. Which accounts actually exist or were authored/changed/destroyed, what do they assert, and what eligible acquisition supports each actor's accessible context?

This is semantic sufficiency, not a separate database or event log. Encounter and the existing knowledge architecture retain their responsibilities. A real account may concern a nonexistent alleged loss or shipment; no matching actual occurrence is required. Actual account creation/destruction and later acquisition do not certify its contents.

Saleable stock, available funds, duty remainder, cash surplus, chosen commercial result, proposed distributions and opportunities are derived from supported facts under explicit bases. Their attributed reports remain F5 and can be wrong. An actor-facing view uses only eligible acquired information; the availability of a privileged calculation does not disclose it. Health/injury, social stance, general legal classification and investigative state are not new Economy truths.

## 3. Money and forms of holding

The minimum proposed slice uses one fictional denomination and counting unit, nonnegative spendable positions and no lending, overdraft, FX or money-creation model. Unpaid duties may persist when funds are zero. This limits the slice rather than prohibiting debt or promising solvency.

Physical cash is a supported quantity in a wallet, till, safe or transit custody. Its amount, physical location, responsible custodian, immediate holder, economic interest and authorized use are separate when consequential. A **bank position** is supported institutionally recognized credited value under a selected account/access basis; it is not cash placed at a virtual coordinate. A **bank statement** is F5 and may misdescribe that position. The minimum abstraction does not simulate bank reserve mechanics or per-note identity.

Deposit/withdrawal changes monetary form/access and associated positions, not sales income. A courier carrying deposit cash has neither paid a wage nor automatically created bank credit. Cash receipt, account credit and acknowledgment can be distinct. The same value cannot remain simultaneously spendable in both forms. Delayed/failed credit requires a supplied basis for any outstanding claim/duty; departed cash is not silently restored. No settlement protocol, ordering or failure guarantee is installed (D01/D10).

Actual funded positions constrain purchases/payroll/payouts. Declared external counterparties/endowments may support outside inflows/outflows at stated precision. A report or derived result cannot mint funds. Cash can serve everyday wages and shadow trade; authored bank-channel requirements may serve tax, insurance and major purchasing. Activity labels do not automatically select permissions or channels.

## 4. Goods, inventory and scoped relations

A product kind describes relevant substitutable properties. A pool represents a supported quantity with location, condition and retained origin distinctions. A specific physical item needs supported individual continuity only when consequential. Item identity is not product identity, and neither is an inventory count. New detail cannot fabricate earlier serials, participants or witnesses.

Actual inventory is a view of F1 holdings/relations. Expected deliveries, reservations/promises, exclusive allocations and recorded counts are distinct. A unit in Player custody is excluded from shelf availability before payment/title resolution. Overlapping promises can remain unmet; they cannot create two exclusive copies. Supported split/merge, coarse transformation, spoilage, consumption and damage preserve relevant quantities/causes and declared outputs/losses without microscopic accounting. Mixing does not erase supported provenance or grant per-unit ancestry beyond what was retained.

Property/economic interest answers entitlement or participation under a supplied basis. Custody can express entrusted responsibility; possession immediate holding. Practical control answers present ability, and an applicable grant answers authorized scope. Keep those distinctions where the case supports them; do not invent fine relations absent support. F1 describes/references their supported state; it does not mint grants, decide general title law or turn a claimed permission into authority. Actual institutional changes must satisfy the relevant Encounter/KeyWrapping standing and scope guards (D02).

Tenancy grants selected access/services, not property ownership. A clerk's authority to transport cash is not authority to spend it. A shareholder's economic participation is not management, delegation, group assent or collective knowledge. A physical taking may occur despite prohibition if admitted by Encounter; legality and awareness remain separate.

## 5. Effects, commercial purpose and required performance

F2 describes the actual occurrence/effect once. F3 qualifies that same supported occurrence against exact required scope and records credited fulfillment; qualification is not another performance or transfer. A service/work occurrence may matter even if it satisfies no duty, is unpaid or is falsely described. Partial, overdue, disputed and impossible can coexist as distinct supported dimensions.

| Commercial use | Distinct basis/purpose retained | Shared economic concerns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sale | Supplied exchange terms, separate goods/service, money and any entitlement conditions | F1 positions/relations, F2 effects, F3 applicability/credit, F4 availability, F5 accounts |
| Gift | Supported gratuitous purpose/authorization and selected interest change; not inferred from absent payment alone | Same positions/movement/authority, without inventing purchase debt |
| Wage | Work/eligibility basis, due scope, payroll funding and actual worker receipt | Same monetary effects and exact performance/credit concerns |
| Debt repayment | Identified outstanding duty and explicit qualifying allocation | Same monetary movement; no automatic credit to every debt |

A transfer is the relevant supported movement/change. A payment is actual monetary performance for a supported payable purpose; a claim that money was payment is separately attributable. Actual movement, purpose/applicability and discharge are not interchangeable. “Transaction” groups related commercial terms/effects; it promises neither atomic execution nor rollback.

Payment without delivery, delivery without payment, false receipt without payment, payment without receipt and partial performance remain representable. Failure does not erase completed effects. Return/refund/compensation/recovery is a new supported effect with its own basis. Same-occurrence acknowledgment cannot repeat an effect; the same required scope cannot receive duplicate credit. A genuine second purchase or later payday remains possible. Cross-duty allocation/overlap, priority, cancellation, substitution and remedies remain D01 rather than silently selected.

## 6. Operations, ownership, commercial result and distribution

F4 combines offerings/configuration, resources/inputs, selected service/storage capacity, expected hours, actual availability and access conditions. Store, restaurant, warehouse, bar/club, housing service, employer, reseller and mixed business are configurations. A conditional opportunity derives from those facts plus relevant resources, terms and eligible actor context; it is not a balance, entitlement, completed service or guaranteed demand.

An operation's identity and operating facts are distinct from its owner/operator/employee and supported management grants. Work, eligibility, wage duty, payroll funding, actual pay and attendance account differ. An absence may be authorized leave or standby; real cash can also be paid under a false attendance description. Neither absence nor discrepancy alone proves fraud.

The Phase 3 cash-flow comparison remains useful but is **insufficient as the sole meaning of business profit**. Retain three different derived questions:

- **Cash surplus/shortfall:** included actual operating receipts less included actual operating payments over a declared interval. Internal cash/bank conversion is not income/cost; restock payment is counted once.
- **Commercial result/profit under a stated basis:** included recognized receipts/value and included incurred/used costs according to an explicitly authored recognition rule. It can differ from cash flow because goods remain held or costs remain unpaid. No general accounting standard, valuation system or tax base is adopted.
- **Distributable amount:** a separately authorized pool under supplied terms/reserves/duties and funding constraints. Neither a profit report nor an interest percentage creates authority or spendable funds.

Illustrative independent paper case: receipts 60, used goods from supported opening stock carrying an authored cost 20, earned unpaid wages 10, and restock payment 50 for goods still held. The declared cash view is 10; a specified basis recognizing the used goods and earned wages gives commercial result 30. These are stipulated views for a future challenge, not a selected universal profit rule or executed result. Neither 10 nor 30 automatically becomes a dividend, tax liability or canonical balance. D02 selects distribution authority; D01/D04 retain duty/priority/tax choices.

Owner injections, acquisition of another owner's interest and transfers among own holdings are not sales revenue. Insurance settlement receipts remain identifiable separately from trade. Shares can support a stated participation calculation; actual distribution requires authority, a defined pool and a funded F2 payout. This supports owner business-profit use without adding a profit or corporate-governance engine.

## 7. Accounts, secret books, tax, insurance and fraud

Official, off-books/secret, private, commercial receipt, manifest, stock count, attendance, tax report and insurance claim are F5 recording contexts/assertions. Authentic creation, official status, internal consistency or secrecy does not certify their commercial contents. A bank position is F1 even though its name includes account; its statement is F5.

The off-books abstraction is optional, physical or abstract as a case supports. It is not mandatory infrastructure, a perfect notebook or a privileged shadow truth tier. Both books may lie differently. Destruction may remove future access to the record; it does not erase effects, protected history or already acquired fragments, nor invent another clue.

An attributed statement about a grant/duty is distinct from a valid current institutional act. Where a selected act truly satisfies KeyWrapping's current-performance, receipt, authority, time and exact-scope guards, its consequence may be admitted by the owning authority. Merely writing “authorized,” forging a permit or repeating a historical acceptance does not perform it. Outward failure feedback must not reveal hidden authority, inventory, aliases or causes.

Tax involves a separately supplied fictional normative/duty/reporting basis, actual economic activity, an attributed report, any authorized assessment and actual payment. Cash surplus/commercial result is not automatically the tax base. Insurance involves policy terms/premium, actual loss, attributed claim, coverage decision, funded payout and later original-goods recovery. Recovery does not duplicate goods, reverse payout, determine salvage or prove initial fraud. Policy-specific disclosure/repayment/interest choices remain D05; normative coverage D04.

Fraud representation combines supported conduct/effects, assertions and any separately supplied authority/mental facts under a selected rule. A discrepancy alone establishes no intent, legal classification, discovery or enforcement. A claim may allege a nonexistent event without creating it. Correcting records cannot pay wages, restore goods or cleanse source.

## 8. Logistics, aggregate activity and consequential detail

Use the local chain declared outside supply -> warehouse -> transit -> business -> use/onward movement. Orders, payment, promise/allocation, dispatch, partial receipt and credited fulfillment are separate. Warehouse is ordinary F4 operation with F1 stock/access. A manifest alone moves nothing. Diverting three of ten transit units leaves supported diverted and remaining portions; later arrival cannot add the original ten again. Any earlier partial arrival remains separately supported. Combat supplies violent outcomes; Economy represents the admitted economic aftermath.

Aggregate background activity represents these same resources/effects at coarse precision. It need not instantiate every shopper, employee purchase, banknote or transaction. It may represent supported quantity/value changes within declared resource/activity/time coverage and outside-counterparty scope, excluding explicit effects. It is not another stock/money engine or replayable schedule.

Increasing detail refines supported present facts and prospective activity while preserving prior changes and consequential exceptions. It does not generate a second sale/wage/shipment or reconstruct an unsupported buyer, serial, route or witness. Known consequential facts cannot be forgotten and called unknown to save attention. If old linkage is unsupported, leave association unresolved; new testimony remains an account. Whether explicit Player trade displaces anonymous demand is a D08 content choice; single counting applies either way. Ordering/promotion and same-occurrence guarantees remain D03/D10 shared questions.

Health/Sleep/Hunger/Hygiene concerns use supplied Encounter condition truth and selected goods/services. Food purchase, acquisition and consumption differ; paid bed/shower access is not successful sleep/hygiene use. Food quality/spoilage, clothing tiers and coarse maintenance/upkeep are content. Housing combines scoped access/storage/services with recurring rent/bills/upkeep, distinct from property ownership and remedies. Keep the owner's contextual clothes/hygiene threshold for richer engagement and basic/emergency/simple-instruction/refusal exceptions. No universal class/trust score follows.

D06 retains owner recovery floor, failure policy and legitimate progression choices. Recommend modest accessible recovery and meaningful legitimate progression, with costs/time/quality tradeoffs; this is not free entitlement or accepted balance. Conditions/injury/substance consequences remain with Encounter/Combat or later authorized scope. Phone display can use eligible acquired context; accepted KW-08 forbids a Player scratchpad, including one renamed freeform notes. Broader authoring requires a separate owner-directed architectural reconciliation; display versus omission remains D07.

Use Encounter time, local hours, employer-specific paydays and prospective authored demand conditions. Funding, wages and later spending are separate; payday supplies no duplicate money. Weekend nightlife need not imply universal higher demand or closed workplaces. Actual staffing/stock/capacity/interruption constrain actual activity. Skipped time never replays covered periods or grants all missed opportunities; unrelated supported charges/duties can still accrue. D08 retains exact cadence, demand displacement, offscreen earning and catch-up policy. A truly later funded return may be legitimate; reopening a report is not a new return.

Real throughput constrains real commerce, not the magnitude of a false revenue claim. No secret truth validator or automatic police alert is introduced.
