## Family structure: retain five, narrow the fourth

No examined household, service, shadow or rhythm case requires a sixth Economy family. Nor is it safe to collapse operations into holdings or obligations: an available washroom, a prospective work shift and a staffed venue can have capacity/availability without merchandise stock or an already formed duty.

The useful correction is to make **F4 operations and availability** the minimum owned meaning. Retain “opportunities” as review terminology for a derived possibility involving operation availability/capacity, F1 resources/access and F3 supplied terms, together with eligible Encounter actor context. This is not a new opportunity balance, universal utility value or guarantee of outcome.

| Family | Minimum meaning needed from these perspectives | Separation that consolidation must preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 Holdings / Interests | Supported resources and orthogonal interests, custody/possession, practical control and scoped grants | Business participation is not operation authority; bodily condition is not a fungible holding; available shelf stock is not a second stock balance. |
| F2 Economic Effects | Supported actual economic performance, movement, use, loss and relevant consequence under Encounter authority | Actual use is not purchased access. A change of cash custody is not income or wage receipt. A service result cannot be supplied merely by labeling a payment. |
| F3 Terms / Duties / Performance | Supplied formation basis, exact required scope, due condition and credited qualifying performance | A schedule is not a duty by itself; a duty is not its due state; due state is not work/payment; payment does not determine every property, legal or outcome question. |
| F4 Operations / Opportunities | Configured offerings, inputs, actual availability, service/storage capacity and selected prospective conditions | It references F1 resources and supplied actor conditions; it does not duplicate them. Expected hours, present access, willingness, actual work, service completion and successful outcome differ. Opportunities are derived possibilities, not entitlements or effects. |
| F5 Attributed Accounts | Supported account authorship/content/existence and eligible access history | Official, private and recipient reports can all lie. Their assertions do not set actual capacity, receipts, title, intent or actor knowledge. Acquisition remains accepted KeyWrapping authority. |

The retained five-family grouping is a proposed allocation of documentary meanings, not a five-class schema or demonstrated mathematical minimum. Actual condition changes and operation interruption remain Encounter-owned facts, even when economically relevant. F4 must not become a catch-all writer for health, injury, permission, willingness, knowledge or outcomes.

## Agent 2: Needs and household perspective

**Finding:** household needs remain content and operation/use specializations if access, actual use and resulting condition remain distinct. The Phase 3 direction is compatible; the main risk is reading “F4 pressure” as a new authoritative needs system.

- Buying a meal changes supported money/goods facts. Eating is a separate supported use; any Hunger/Health consequence is supplied by Encounter. An aggregate household-consumption allowance must exclude a meal already explicitly consumed.
- A rented room offers selected rest, hygiene, charging, storage and hosting access. Rent payment, tenancy entitlement, current entry, facility availability and actual use differ. Paying for a bed is not sleeping, and buying a good home is not guaranteed safety or social success.
- A broken charger can remove charging while rest/storage remain. Modeling every housing service as one all-or-nothing “housing satisfied” state would erase an owner-intended meaningful distinction. This needs content-specific availability, not a housing subsystem.
- Health/Sleep/Hunger/Hygiene, food/clothing tiers and maintenance quality remain supplied Encounter/content concerns. Economy can express goods/services, time cost and later burden; it must not double-apply impairment already represented by Encounter/Combat.
- Contextual clothes/hygiene standards for richer engagement retain the owner's basic-transaction, emergency, basic-question, instruction and refusal exceptions. Reject an interpretation where poor presentation universally prevents buying food, seeking aid or receiving a refusal. These exceptions do not by themselves choose a free recovery entitlement or terminal-failure policy.
- Medicine and abstract substance recovery can be purchased/used as ordinary goods/services. The drug label supplies no medical rates, injury reversal, guaranteed Combat effect or automatic disclosure of dependence.
- A phone is resource/capability/access content. Device availability, eligible acquired backlog and already learned information differ. Replacing or losing the device cannot reset learned references; device access alone cannot acquire every stored/incoming message.

**Required counterexample:** a tired, poorly dressed Player has enough cash for either a basic meal or improved meeting presentation. The basic sale remains available under the supplied terms, richer engagement may remain unavailable, buying the food does not itself consume it, and a later skipped interval does not consume/charge that same meal again. Whether an additional recovery route is guaranteed remains owner choice D06.

## Agent 6: Shadow economy perspective

**Finding:** ordinary/shadow reuse survives. No distinct criminal money, stolen-stock physics, front-business truth, laundering conversion, vice inventory or fence engine is warranted. Classification, secrecy, disclosure and authoring can differ while resources, performance and accounts remain common.

| Concrete use | Reused composition | Distinction that must remain visible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store or restaurant | F4 offerings/capacity + F1 stock/access + F2 payment/use/service + F3 terms + optional F5 records | A restaurant can transform/use goods and perform service; menu availability is not a completed meal or guaranteed social interaction. |
| Warehouse or delivery node | F1 stock/custody + F4 capacity/access + F2 departure/transit/receipt + F3 required delivery | Expected delivery or manifest is not stock arrival. Hidden storage uses the same relations. |
| Bar or club | F4 venue/service capacity + F1 resources/grants + F2 cover/food/drink/service + F3 supplied terms | Paid admission creates only the supported access/performance, not friendship, an introduction, private knowledge or consent. |
| Drug goods/distribution | Ordinary goods/pools, storage, movement, payment and fallible accounts | Product content and separately supplied normative/condition meanings vary. No drug-specific logistics or medical authority is imported. |
| Vice service | F4 service availability/capacity + F2 supported service/payment + F3 terms, with optional F5 accounts | Merchandise is optional. Participation, consent, coercion, gambling result and injury need the proper separately supplied authority. People are not goods. |
| Fence | Ordinary reseller specialization | Product fit, finite funds, storage/service capacity, access, willingness and downstream opportunity differ. Refusal/partial buying leaves unsold goods; no guaranteed universal buyer. |
| Organized theft | Ordinary retained goods, custody changes, supported payments, scoped operations/access and accounts | Organization does not create collective ownership, assent, secrecy or knowledge. Combat supplies violence; Laws classifies; Economy preserves supported economic aftermath. No crime mission or coordination engine is inferred. |
| Mixed business / hidden interest / no-show payroll | F1 actual interest/resources + F2 real movement + F3 duty basis + F4 real operation + F5 divergent reports | Real ownership, authority, performed work and claimed attendance can differ. An off-books report is not privileged truth. |
| Laundering/concealed proceeds | Ordinary actual source/movement, supported access and divergent asserted source/disclosure | Mixing/deposit does not erase provenance. Institutional acceptance or a special game benefit needs a selected recipient/acquisition/decision basis. |

**Required counterexample:** a tiny venue has supported cash from a concealed source and a false report of enormous sales. Real stock/staff/capacity constrain real trade; they cannot logically prevent the false account from claiming arbitrary receipts. The report creates no funds and automatically informs no accountant/police actor. Its eventual payoff or cost is D11; future enforcement is not a present balancing cost.

**Qualification:** “finite funds/capacity/downstream access” is not a demand that every buyer carry a privileged universal acceptance flag or publish rejection reasons. Actual constraints and actor-visible reasons remain separately supported. Acquired evidence, willingness and permission remain distinct. A matching product need not force the counterparty to buy.

## Agent 7: Time and rhythm perspective

**Finding:** local rhythms should remain authored expectations and prospective conditions under Encounter time. They neither create new money nor substitute for actual operations. The Phase 3 architecture is sound if its recommended staggering is not mistaken for an accepted universal calendar.

1. Employer payroll funding, a particular worker's paid wages and later purchases are different effects. An aggregate customer boundary must not independently count spending already represented explicitly by that worker.
2. A known payday, expected opening or weekend window does not disclose actual cash withdrawal, staff presence, stock or a secret delivery. Player-facing notice needs eligible acquisition.
3. Closure can suppress walk-in sales while rent remains due, stored cash remains, an authorized delivery arrives or a separately supported channel operates. “Closed” must have the selected operation/channel scope, not indiscriminately freeze every economic fact.
4. Busy/slow conditions are prospective, local and capacity-constrained. A busy club and quiet/closed pharmacy can coexist. No universal weekend multiplier, guaranteed demand or crime pulse follows.
5. Skipped time cannot replay covered periods or complete interrupted performance. Already completed effects remain; unperformed expectations remain unperformed. Genuine new funded earnings may occur under selected offscreen content, so waiting itself is not automatically an exploit.
6. A new explicit event must either be outside the aggregate's scope or replace/exclude an identified overlapping portion. Choose a simple fixture exclusion before later execution; displacement of anonymous demand is owner content, not a conservation theorem.
7. A coarse daily total cannot later answer an unsupported exact-hour/customer question. Local opportunity can become explicit prospectively without manufacturing old participants or infinite history.

**Required counterexample:** a store closes for the first half of a selected trading period, then opens. The retained aggregate covers only actual supported trade; a worker's explicit purchase is excluded. Rent and an already due wage remain separate. Re-entering the venue or advancing the same period again adds no second sale or payday. The exact cadence and missed-opportunity policy remain D08.

## Agent 11: Gameplay and pressure perspective

**Finding:** five families can describe the intended economy; description alone does not establish a playable loop. Preserve the difference between semantic completeness and intended return, legibility, chore burden, legitimate progression or criminal risk/reward.

### Cash flow, profit and distribution

The Phase 3 main text correctly separates these, but the separation should be a named requirement wherever business ownership/profit is claimed:

| Question | Proposed treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Where is spendable money? | Supported F1 holdings and restrictions/access. Cash on site, bank position and owner wealth differ. |
| What happened to operating cash in this period? | A derived, scoped included-receipts-minus-included-payments result. Label it cash surplus/shortfall; exclude own-account conversions and owner injections from sales. |
| Was the operation profitable? | A derived result under an explicitly supplied recognition/basis for receipts and costs. The minimum fully settled fixture may use a simple cash basis; do not silently generalize it to unpaid wages, inventory timing, taxes or all future businesses. No accounting-profit balance or full accounting engine is required. |
| What is distributable? | A separately supplied authorized distribution basis, available funds, selected duties/reserves and relevant grants. No universal creditor priority is installed. |
| What did an owner receive? | A new funded F2 effect under the applicable authority/terms; a percentage or reported profit does not pay anyone. |

**Counterexample:** sales cash can arrive while wages remain due and unpaid. A positive cash surplus is compatible with outstanding wage performance; it does not settle that duty or automatically authorize distributing all cash. Conversely, moving owner funds into a till raises its balance without sales or profit. A manager can operate a store without owning an interest; an investor can own an interest without a grant to empty the till. These distinctions do not require separate “business ownership” and “business operation” engines.

### Pressure and opportunity

- Preserve a modest-recovery and viable-legitimate-progression recommendation, while leaving guarantees, terminal deprivation, progression pace and passive ownership growth to D06. A repair for an unintended poverty trap cannot quietly revoke the owner's contextual presentation standard.
- Compare actual current time, resources, access and missed opportunities in legitimate/shadow content. Do not claim unavailable future investigation or injury mechanisms balance a current criminal route.
- A home may be worth paying for because it bundles dependable authored services and saves travel/time. It need not grant a global status buff or perfect safety; free alternatives and premium convenience still require owner-selected content.
- A venue or workplace can create occasions for acquired dialogue, references and disputes. Payment cannot buy trust, belief, consent or secret identity joins automatically.
- Waiting through real supported periods can legitimately yield ownership returns. The architecture must distinguish that from repeated presentation of the same effect; intended growth remains owner policy and later evidence.
