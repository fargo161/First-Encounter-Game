# Core invariants proposed for owner acceptance

**Status:** REVIEW-READY EXTRACTION FROM UNCHANGED PHASE 4 — PROPOSED, NOT OWNER-ACCEPTED ECONOMY ARCHITECTURE  
**Date:** 2026-10-23, America/New_York

This register groups the minimum semantic constraints that future Economy work would preserve if the owner adopts the [acceptance statement](PROPOSED_ACCEPTANCE_STATEMENT.md). It adds no primitive, policy, runtime guarantee or authority. “Invariant” identifies a required semantic property of the candidate; it is not evidence that software enforces it. Accepted external constraints are already binding independently of the pending Economy decision. The [Phase 4 candidate](../phase-4/ECONOMY_SUBSTRATE_V0_1_RECONCILED.md), [family structure](../phase-4/FAMILY_STRUCTURE_FOR_AUDIT.md) and [boundaries](../phase-4/CROSS_SYSTEM_BOUNDARIES.md) remain the source text.

| I01 | **One authoritative history.** Economic positions, effects, record events and credited scope specialize one Encounter-admitted identity/state/effects/time/history authority. Families do not authorize independent writers. | Candidate §§1–2; boundaries B01; accepted Encounter ownership | One shipment exists in a background engine and again in an explicit engine. |

Reports and calculations cannot mint spendable value. | Candidate §§2–3/6; F1/F2 | Owner contribution is counted as sales, or profit calculation credits money. |
| I04 | **Cash, credit and claims differ.** Physical location/custody, credited bank position, pending-credit claim and bank statement remain distinct. Conversion cannot leave the same value spendable in both forms. | Candidate §3; F1/F2/F5 | Deposit retains spendable cash and immediately adds identical spendable bank credit; a statement creates the credit. |
| I05 | **Supported goods continuity.** Split/merge, transformation, consumption/spoilage/destruction, diversion/resale/recovery account for supported quantities and causes/declared outputs or losses. | I07 | **Requirements and actual occurrences differ.** Exact basis/terms/activation/due and qualifying/credited scope remain separate. | I08 | **Credit once to the same required scope.** Preserve partial, late, disputed and remaining dimensions; retry/acknowledgment cannot repeat an effect or credit. A genuine later supported occurrence remains distinguishable. General cross-duty allocation remains open. | Candidate §5; D01/D10 | Reopening a paid duty creates more pay, or a single fulfilled flag erases an unpaid remainder. |
| I09 | **Payment, delivery and purpose differ.** Monetary performance, goods/custody/interest transfer and discharge need their respective bases. Sale/gift/wage/repayment reuse effects without losing purpose. Refund/return/compensation/recovery is a new supported effect. No atomicity/rollback is promised. | Candidate §5 | Receipt proves delivery/payment; failure rewrites earlier payment; missing payment turns a transfer into a gift. |
| I10 | **Result is not distribution.** Cash flow, commercial result under a stated basis, available funds, authorized distributable pool, approval and funded actual payout remain distinct. | I11 | **Conditions are prospective, effects actual.** Operation identity/configuration, ownership/management, expected versus actual availability, inputs/capacity/access and derived opportunities differ. | I12 | **Accounts are attributed assertions.** Actual account authorship/change/destruction and asserted content differ. Official/private/off-books accounts may disagree with actuality and each other, including claims of nonexistent events. No account has privileged truth. | Candidate §§2/7; F5 | False claim requires a canonical false loss; destroying the ledger undoes actual wages or source. |
| I13 | **Claims do not supply authority; valid acts need guards.** An assertion of a grant/duty is not its valid current performance. A genuine institutional act may have a consequence only under the owning authority's actual standing, receipt, time and exact-scope conditions. | Candidate §§4/7; B06; accepted KeyWrapping authority/performance | Forged permit grants authority; alternatively every genuinely valid current act is treated as inert text. |
| I14 | **Truth, evidence and acquired knowledge differ.** Retained provenance, record availability and actor acquisition are distinct. Every actor-facing label, alias link, detail or diagnostic requires eligible acquired scope; ownership/employment/co-location supplies no automatic knowledge. | Candidate §§2/7/10; B04/B05; accepted KeyWrapping | Secret ledger or canonical source automatically informs police, accountants or the Player; a failure message leaks hidden stock/authority. |
| I15 | **One aggregate/explicit coverage.** Coarse and explicit activity concern the same positions/effects. Declared resource/activity/time/outside scope, exclusions and consequential exceptions prevent duplicate counting. Refinement/re-entry cannot replay covered periods or fabricate past precision. | Candidate §8; B11 | Explicit Player sales and aggregate sales include the same goods; revisiting regenerates a shipment/payday. |
| I16 | **Source survives ordinary/shadow reuse at supported precision.** Drugs, vice, fencing, hidden interests, mixed businesses and laundering compose the same families. Mixing/deposit/relabel/resale cannot erase supported provenance or invent unsupported per-unit ancestry; secret books are no second economy. | Candidate §§4/9; B12 | A clean-money type deletes source, or a mixed-pool partial outflow invents a favorable exact residual allocation. |
| I17 | **Finite support preserves consequential continuity.** Retain required causal support, exact active terms/credits, repeat prevention, aggregate coverage/exceptions and applicable protected prefix within a declared envelope. Closure, destruction, summary or lowered salience permits no deletion that enables unsupported continuation. Unknown old precision remains unknown. | Candidate §10; D03/D10; accepted finite-history qualifications | Forget old payout and treat its retry as new; presume safe compaction or unlimited history. |
| I18 | **Economic facts do not perform other systems' reasoning.** Separate normative basis/assessment, loss/claim/coverage/payout/recovery, information acquisition/response, violent outcomes and organizational authority.
