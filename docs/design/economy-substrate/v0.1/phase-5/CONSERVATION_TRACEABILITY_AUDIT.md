## Conservation reasoning

The candidate supplies a coherent place for each required question without requiring competing economic truths. F1 supports positions and separate relations; F2 describes admitted actual effects; F3 retains supplied requirements and qualifies/credits the **same** F2 occurrence; F4 supports prospective operations/capacity; F5 retains attributed descriptions and their actual artifact context. These are roles within Encounter authority, not five independent stores. The family structure explicitly forbids replacing the internal distinctions with one status (FAMILY_STRUCTURE_FOR_AUDIT.md:18-28).

The relevant test is whether a bad continuation can comply with the current requirements. Paying a second time by reopening a report contradicts the once-occurrence rule. Spending the same converted value as cash and bank credit contradicts the form rule. A false invoice can exist without creating a purchase, so there is no structural need to fabricate its target. A coarse pool can preserve contributions without assigning unsupported serial ancestry. A partial duty can remain due/overdue/disputed independently of already completed effects. The model can therefore represent the required hard cases without inventing another event, resource, authority or knowledge system.

This reasoning does not prove all possible implementations conform. It establishes that the current family separations and cross-cutting obligations are sufficient to reject the specified documentary counterexamples at the claimed level. A future representation must establish position/effect/credit consistency under one admitted history; the present proposal does not assume five writers somehow converge.

## Required twelve-domain challenge result

All twelve domains pass documentary review. The [coverage matrix](COVERAGE_MATRIX.md) records the exact guard locations, every required P4 group, concrete challenges and still-unclaimed outcomes. The seven consolidated verdicts are in [CONSERVATION_VERDICTS.md](CONSERVATION_VERDICTS.md).

1. Money cannot arise from statements, duty balances, profit, reports or distribution percentages. Conversion excludes dual spendability; actual payroll/refund/insurance/distribution effects remain separately funded and once represented.
2. Goods kind, pool and consequential item answer different questions. Partition, transformation, use and loss retain supported quantities/causes; diverted/resold/recovered units continue original goods.
3. F3 qualification cannot create a second F2 work, service, delivery or payment. Same required scope receives credit once; genuine later effects remain possible.
4. Due, transfer, payment, delivery, receipt and discharge remain independent. Refund/recovery/compensation adds an effect with basis rather than deleting the prior one.
5. The ten-unit partial shipment preserves two received, three diverted and five remaining under supplied order. Detail increase adds neither stock nor a fabricated precise past.
6. Aggregate and explicit descriptions cover one economic history with exclusions/reconciled exceptions. Period re-entry, time skipping and promotion cannot replay covered effects.
7. Official, private and off-books records remain fallible. A phantom claim requires no actual target event; account correction/destruction cannot undo real effects.
8. Mixing, deposits, resale and relabeling preserve supported actual provenance, independently of discoverable evidence and actor knowledge. Evidence destruction cannot delete protected support.
9. Possession, custody, control, entitlement and scoped authority remain separate. Shareholding supplies neither management nor collective knowledge; an asserted grant needs actual accepted validity guards.
10. Loss, claim, coverage, payout and recovery remain distinct. A funded false-claim payout can be real without making the claim true; recovery neither reverses it nor proves fraud.
11. Ordinary/shadow reuse keeps the same resource/effect rules. A fence has no invented stock, funds or guaranteed demand; a secret book has no privileged truth.
12. Finite support cannot be obtained by deleting needed terms, credits, repeat-prevention evidence, consequential exceptions or protected history. Unsupported old precision stays unavailable; indefinite campaigns remain unclaimed.
