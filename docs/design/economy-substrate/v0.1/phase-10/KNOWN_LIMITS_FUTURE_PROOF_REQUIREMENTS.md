## Gameplay, content, balance and experience limits

| Compatible possibility or untested claim | Meaning and required future basis |
| --- | --- |
| Policy-dependent insurance windfalls can exist | Funded payout plus recovery of originals plus funded resale can benefit the claimant under compatible coverage/title/no-repayment terms. New repeated cycles need new eligibility, resources and opportunity. This is not duplication and is not established as desirable. D02/D04/D05 must supply the actual terms before a dependent reward/fraud claim. |
| No-show and false-report transfers can be genuinely profitable | A real payer can transfer money without work; a supplied assessment or recipient decision can act on a false account. The account itself creates neither work, funds nor lower liability. No automatic detection or future enforcement cost may be counted as current balance. D01/D04/D05/D11 and actual recipient/funding/access scope matter. |
| Passive ownership can compound | Genuinely later funded operation and separately authorized distributions can finance more interests/capacity. Shares and profit reports create no funds or management, and old distributions cannot replay. Growth horizon, effort, costs, offscreen policy and intended progression remain D02/D06/D08 content choices. |
| Crime can outperform work under some authored values | Ordinary mechanics, finite fence funds and preserved source do not guarantee competitive wages. A finite willing buyer may still offer more return for selected time than work. Comparisons need a common bounded horizon and present funding, time, access, input, liquidity, capacity and opportunity costs. No future police/Combat cost may be assumed. D06/D08/D11 and authored values remain open. |
| Needs/recovery loops can be too easy or too harsh | Food, sleep, hygiene, clothing, medicine, housing and phone access are representable; actual health/social effects remain supplied elsewhere. Basic/emergency presentation exceptions do not fund recovery. Content can produce terminal deprivation, trivial upkeep, progression locks or chores. D06/D08/D09 and real access/time/content choices must precede recoverability or pressure claims. |
| Payday/weekend/offscreen rewards can change strategy | Prospective conditions create no automatic wages, sales or repeated opportunity. Genuine later funded activity is possible. Cadence, demand displacement, skipped-time costs, offscreen earnings and the appeal of waiting remain D08 content policy; no exact multipliers or universal schedule is selected. |
| Player legibility, playability and fun are untested | Internal clarity supplies no proof that Players understand rewards, payment/service gaps, ownership/access or profit/distribution, or that upkeep creates meaningful choices. Future observed scenes must use actor-permitted feedback, without exposing hidden canonical diagnostics. No Player study or playable Economy fixture exists in this lineage. |

## Effect on the owner decision

The owner can accept the bounded architecture while leaving these claims unestablished and residual choices open. Acceptance would preserve the separations needed to select and test a future scope; it would not certify all content, implement its guarantees or guarantee a recovery route, fair income comparison, enjoyable upkeep, final balance or full integration. Only a later explicit work order can select a fixture, authorize any implementation or define a new acceptance claim. **No such work begins in Phase 10.**
