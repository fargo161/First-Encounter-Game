# Final proposed family structure for audit

**Date:** 2026-10-23, America/New_York  
**Status:** PHASE 4 CANDIDATE / FIVE DOCUMENTARY FAMILIES / NOT FINAL OWNER-ACCEPTED ARCHITECTURE

This is the structure selected by the lead after cross-specialist reconciliation. It refines the [Phase 3 model](../phase-3/ECONOMY_SUBSTRATE_V0_1.md), rather than treating its five labels as indivisible primitives. Family ownership below means responsibility for economic meaning in this proposal; **Encounter retains all admitted world-state/history authority**. None is an independent engine, store or runtime class.

## Family responsibilities and required internal roles

| Family | Minimum semantic responsibility | Required distinct roles | Specialization/content or derived concern | Authority dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1: resource positions and economic relations | What relevant resource quantity/item or credited monetary position is supported, and which scoped economic relationships apply | Resource kind/condition and quantity/item position; separately supported interests, custody/possession, practical control and referenced grants | Wallet/till/safe, bank position, stock pool, rental access and share participation specialize the concern. Available/saleable amount is a derived view | Encounter identity/state; accepted KeyWrapping reference and actual-authority separations. Standing/title/grant rules must be supplied, not invented here |
| F2: actual economic effects | Actual resource movement/use/transformation/loss, supported service/work occurrence and associated consequences admitted by Encounter | Genuine occurrence; its actual economic effect scope and supported cause/source/destination | Sale, gift, wage, repayment, payout and diversion are uses with different bases, not independent effect engines. An episode grouping is a view, not atomicity | Encounter admitted effects, ordering, identity and protected history; native examples do not install a generic Economy effect protocol |
| F3: required performance and fulfillment | Identify supplied exact requirement/duty basis and evaluate supported performance against its required scope | Basis/exact terms; formation/activation/due dimensions; qualification and credited scope of an existing actual occurrence | Wage, rent, supplier and statutory bases differ; recurring occurrences, payroll grouping, remaining amounts and summary status derive from these facts | Accepted KeyWrapping terms, authority and performance invariants; Governance for open formation/allocation/remedy semantics; Laws for normative bases |
| F4: operating conditions and capacity | Reusable authored operation configuration and supported current availability/inputs/capacity/access conditions | Operation/configuration distinct from operator identity, ownership and management grants; expectations distinct from actual availability | Store, restaurant, warehouse, household service, club, fence, mixed trade are configurations. An opportunity is a conditional/derived possibility, not another balance or completed act | Encounter time/condition/outcome authority; scoped grants from their actual authority; no new health, social, demand or opportunity truth engine |
| F5: attributed economic accounts | Describe an attributed assertion/report and its supported artifact/communication context | Account/record existence and authorship; asserted subject/content; eligible audience acquisition supplied by existing knowledge architecture | Official/private/off-books accounts, manifests, receipts, tax/insurance reports and assessments share this concern. A bank statement is an account; a credited bank position belongs to F1 | Encounter actual record/communication history; accepted KeyWrapping claims/reference/acquisition constraints. Information discovery and classification authority remain external |

These internal roles must remain distinguishable even if a later authorized implementation chooses different storage. They are semantic obligations, not proposed fields or serialized record types.

## What the pressure test changes

**F1 was safe only as an envelope.** A single owned quantity would lose entrusted cash, consignment, tenancy and partial commercial transfer. Positions and relations therefore remain explicit internal roles. Ownership does not contain custody; practical control does not contain authority. A credited bank position cannot be reduced to physical cash located at a bank. A product kind is not stock, and a stock pool is not a serially identifiable item. No new top-level family is needed to preserve these distinctions, but combining their values into one status is prohibited by this candidate.

**F2/F3 must not each create a performance.** The supported delivery/work/payment is one actual occurrence under Encounter. F3 refers to it to assess compatibility, quantity, time and exact required scope. Qualifying or crediting it does not deliver the goods, pay again, create a second work shift or prove a statement true. One occurrence can have several economic consequences; a duty can be partially credited and still overdue or disputed. General cross-duty overlap remains open.

**F4 was too broad if opportunities were authoritative outcomes.** Narrow its ownership to configuration/operating conditions and supported capacity/availability. Derive an opportunity from those conditions and the selected view. An offering is not necessarily a communicated binding offer; a schedule is not actual staffing; paid access is not service use, health recovery, social assent or acquired information. Needs truth, consent, gambling outcomes and Combat stay outside. This removes duplication without removing business or household content.

**F5 must not become an economic knowledge subsystem.** A real false invoice has actual creation history but need not correspond to an actual purchase. No phantom purchase is added to F2 to give the claim a target. An account's claim of authority is not a valid grant. If a communication genuinely performs an authorized institutional act under accepted KeyWrapping guards, its supported effect comes through that authority; F5 does not make every sentence inert or every official sentence true. Actor acquisition remains separately supported, including audience-safe labels and failures.

## Required distinction matrix

| Distinction challenged | Reconciled treatment | Concrete shortcut rejected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property/economic interest / custody / possession / control | Orthogonal scoped F1 relations; entrusted responsibility and immediate holding differ when supported | Clerk carrying till cash becomes owner, receives wages or gains spending power |
| Monetary holding / cash location / bank position | F1 common quantity reasoning with different form/access meaning; conversion uses supported F2 effects | Deposit leaves the same value spendable as both cash and bank credit |
| Product type / aggregate stock / specific item | Kind describes; pool counts; item continuity needs retained support | Later dispute invents a serial or earlier buyer for an anonymous unit |
| Obligation / due / performance / actual transfer | F3 requirement/due/credit refer to actual F2 occurrence; dimensions stay separate | Due time creates payment, receipt proves performance or one fulfilled flag erases lateness |
| Payment / goods transfer | Monetary performance/purpose differs from goods/custody/interest effects | Goods and payment automatically roll back together |
| Sale / gift / wage / debt repayment | Context/basis/required performance differs while movements reuse F1/F2/F3 | Identical amount/recipient makes every transfer a sale or settles every duty |
| Actual occurrence / official / off-books / claim | Actuality under Encounter; plural attributed F5 assertions may disagree or allege nonexistent events | Secret book becomes privileged truth or claim creates the incident |
| Loss / claim / payout / recovery | Separate actual effects, assertions and policy-based duties/interests | Recovery duplicates stock, reverses money or proves earlier dishonesty |
| Operation / business ownership | F4 operating facts, F1 economic participation/grants, F3 distribution basis, F2 funded payout | Share purchase grants management or a percentage of gross cash automatically |
| Background aggregate / explicit consequence | Same resources/effects, declared coverage/exclusions and consequential exceptions | A second economy or another past sale appears on elaboration |
| Provenance / evidence / knowledge | Actual retained support; available evidence/account; actor-specific acquired scope differ | Canonical lineage or counterparty status grants actor knowledge |
| Legal classification / economic fact | Supported facts distinct from scoped normative basis and attributed/authorized assessment | Missing stock proves theft, absent worker proves fraud or classification triggers police |
| Shadow specialization / ordinary primitive | Reuse all five; supported source, access, authority and classification may differ | Stolen stock, illicit cash or laundering uses different physical accounting |

## Rejected structural alternatives

| Alternative | Why not selected | Distinction preserved instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Split F1 into separate top-level resource and interest families | Plausible organization, but no additional economic authority or reusable mechanism is demonstrated; would not by itself prevent mixing balances and rights | Mandatory positions-versus-relations roles inside F1, with independent support and counterexamples |
| Collapse F3 into F2 | Loses unperformed/conditional duties, late-but-paid state and exact credit scope | Actual occurrence versus requirement/qualification relation |
| Collapse F4 into holdings and effects | Loses prospective operating conditions, constrained services and local availability without inventing events | Operation configuration/capacity distinct from resource position and actual performance |
| Separate opportunities, payroll, profit, warehouses, shadow or laundering into families | These are derived views or compositions/content; separate engines would duplicate resource/effect/duty concerns | Narrower F4; explicit result basis; common F1-F5 compositions |
| Collapse F5 into actual events, or add a private truth ledger | Cannot express false claims, nonexistent alleged events, incompatible records or unequal acquisition | Attributed assertion versus admitted actuality and separate acquired audience |
| Add aggregate/provenance/retention as a second authoritative history | Those constrain representation/support of the same facts; a new history would create conflict | Coverage, supported precision and finite protected support across families |

The proposal retains five envelopes because removing another loses a distinct question, while splitting an envelope adds no demonstrated responsibility beyond the mandatory roles. This is design reasoning for audit, not proof that no other organization can work. The [before/after record](CHANGES_FROM_PHASE_3.md), specialist memos and [decision register](RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md) preserve disagreements, conditions and unresolved authority rather than claim final acceptance.
