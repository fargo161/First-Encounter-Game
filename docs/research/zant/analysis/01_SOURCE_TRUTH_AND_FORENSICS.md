# Source truth and asset forensics

Analysis date: October 18, 2026. Source art is unchanged. This is an evidence assessment and authoring recommendation, with no asset conversion or production implementation.

The handoff is sufficient to justify a small proof, but its inventories and rebuilt references are not a ready modular library. The strongest evidence is the preserved source cast plus a technically inspectable frontal face proof. The most consequential new finding is that current catalogs still contain cropped objects, mannequin fragments, guide marks and category leakage. File existence and RGBA mode do not establish clean separation.

## Isolation and metadata risks

All 44 clothing sheets have binary alpha, yet **38/44 touch at least one canvas edge**, 28 touch the bottom and 26 the left. Edge contact alone does not prove a garment is clipped; visual checks establish the actual cases: C020's shoes are truncated while its complete kit plate still shows them; C001/C020/C034 retain cropped gray mannequin arm fragments; C001/C033 retain guide-box lines; C027/C028 clothing sheets include cropped gear along the bottom; C010's clothing sheet mixes multiple gear items. These plates require selective recovery from fuller kit/source images and manual validation, not blind connected-component export. Recorded alpha blob counts are geometric blobs, never counts of semantic garments.

The gear repair manifest accurately limits its claim to 25 catalogs: five complete, two partial, 18 empty, 13 recovered isolated items and 30 declared items without source isolation. Current alpha scanning finds 19 empty files overall (the repaired 18 plus C042) and 25 nonempty. Only seven nonempty files are in that repair list: C005,C007,C014,C015,C026,C029,C043. Among the other18 nonempty older files,17 contain confirmed clothing/footwear/guide fragments; e.g. C001 contains shoe/pants fragments and a guide box, C020 skirt/coat/shoes and a guide box, C010 clothing and boots. C028 appears gear-only, but its helmet and vest are clipped at the top. “V03.2 repaired” must not be read as “all 44 gear catalogs clean.”

V03.1 intentionally contains RGB dilation beneath alpha-zero for filtering. Raw RGBA previews may expose these otherwise invisible colors; diagnostic plates composite with alpha. No visible exact #FF00FF pixels remain in rebuilt/calibration images under the audit's alpha>0 test, but softened pink/purple fringe and small source green edges remain visually relevant. “No exact magenta” is not a general despill or edge-quality certificate. A broad near-magenta flag is not proof either, since legitimate purple garments exist.

Structured C001 garment metadata still says polo/plaid trousers/loafers while art shows tee/tracksuit/sneakers. The current JSON therefore supplies IDs, resolvable paths, source hashes and candidate references more reliably than descriptive garment labels. The V02 identity map's 44 legacy `masters/character_###_neutral_rgba.png` paths do not exist in this delivery; three historical filenames remain unresolved as recorded in pre-flight. Repeated README despill sections, old QA `visual_review_required`, and the master's face-part reference to Parts4–6 are historical text problems. Actual face materials are Parts5–7. Do not silently “repair” provenance by claiming similarly named files are identical.

## Status and proof consequence

Usable unchanged **as evidence**: original masters/references, kit plates, calibration/fit references, manifest/provenance records and the supplied face proof. Potentially recoverable source-only art: isolated-looking garment/gear regions after selective cleanup and inspection. Calibration-only: gray mannequins and historical fit exemplars. Required new/derived art: four canonical views, hidden continuations, garment-aware whole-limb states, independent hands/feet/head interfaces, clean layered garments, palette masks, and multi-view facial/hair packages. No current file family establishes these collectively.

**Recommend C001 for the first proof**, because its intact source, full kit plate and relatively close reconstruction isolate a useful tee/jacket/stripe/pants/footwear problem without long-coat or protective-stack cost. It remains provisional pending head identity/face findings: a separate Goose head proof must not be mislabeled C001 or stretched onto his neck without identity approval. C033 is a good next slim layered-pattern case; C034 adds a visibly different body and plaid; C020 and C028 should remain later long-garment and equipment challenges. C001's dirty catalogs make the proof more informative, but demand small selective authoring from original authority rather than automated conversion.

The analysis is sufficient to specify this proof. Missing turnaround and isolated geometry are build-stage art requirements, not missing-package blockers.
