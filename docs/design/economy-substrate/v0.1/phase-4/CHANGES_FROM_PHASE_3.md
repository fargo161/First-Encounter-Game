## Specialist recommendations and lead dispositions

The [material/performance memo](reconciliation/MATERIAL_AND_PERFORMANCE.md), [household/shadow/rhythm memo](reconciliation/HOUSEHOLD_SHADOW_AND_RHYTHM.md) and [authority/continuity memo](reconciliation/AUTHORITY_AND_CONTINUITY.md) preserve separately labeled role passes and source classes. Their common condition for five families is adopted: required semantic roles must remain explicit. Their counterexamples are incorporated into R01-R14 and the proof set, not claimed as executed findings.

Alternatives considered include splitting F1 positions/interests or F3 duties/credit into additional top-level families. The lead declines those organizational splits because the internal roles answer the demonstrated concerns without adding an authority or mechanism; an independent split remains a possible later organizational choice if an audit finds an actual composition failure. The rejected alternative of collapsing F3 into effects would lose unpaid/conditional/late duties. Collapsing F5 into events would lose false and nonexistent alleged subjects.

On proof organization, the gameplay/material perspectives allowed retaining nine headings with mandatory variants, while the authority/specification perspective recommended isolating four challenge bundles and the business-result subcase. The lead chooses fourteen named groups so every bundle is separately assessable. This changes organization and explicit coverage, not evidence of success.

In a bounded authoring follow-up, the material/performance and authority/continuity participants checked incorporation in the lead package. The material participant requested that the candidate's profit example explicitly identify supported opening stock for the used-goods cost; that clarification is incorporated. Both reported no further actionable contradiction within their reconciliation remit. These are participant follow-ups, not cold independent review or later audit findings.

All eleven decision cards retain genuine residuals; safe architectural subquestions close in the [reconciled register](RECONCILED_DECISION_REGISTER.md). Keeping owner creative choices open is intentional, not a failure to reconcile. No external Governance sign-off is claimed. **Phase 4 ends at owner review before Phase 5.**
