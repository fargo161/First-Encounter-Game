## D01: Duty basis, allocation, priority and remedies — PARTIALLY CLOSED

**Closed for candidate:** differentiated formation bases share exact requirement/due/qualifying/credited concerns. F3 qualifies the same F2 occurrence. Partial/late/disputed are independent dimensions. Same required scope cannot receive duplicate credit. **Basis:** accepted KeyWrapping C07-C09; roles 3/5/8/9/10. Universal assent for statutory duties and independent duty engines are unnecessary/incompatible shortcuts.

**Remaining choice / authority:** owner and shared Governance/KeyWrapping, plus Laws for statutory bases, must supply exact activation, authority to allocate/reallocate, cross-duty overlap, priority, cancellation, substitution and remedies where claimed.

**Compatible alternatives:** A explicit duty-specific basis and disjoint allocation; B explicitly allowed shared/overlapping credit or netting under supplied terms. **Recommended default:** A in the first bounded fixture, without promoting it to universal law. **Consequences:** A is simpler and prevents accidental reuse but is narrower; B supports richer settlements and needs exact overlap/priority semantics. **Specific decision needed:** which bases and allocations are allowed and who has standing. Until supplied, audit can challenge separation/duplicate-credit requirements but cannot assert a generic priority/remedy outcome.
