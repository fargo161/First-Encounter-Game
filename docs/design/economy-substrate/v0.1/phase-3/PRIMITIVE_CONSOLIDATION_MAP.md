## Reduction decisions

Separate cash/goods families were considered; F1 instead shares position/scope reasoning while preserving form and item distinctions. A separate interest family was considered; explicit orthogonal relations inside F1 preserve its meaning with fewer top-level families. Separate aggregate/provenance/retention families were considered; those concerns qualify the same facts/effects and constrain their support, so they do not become another world-state authority.

No standalone sale, payroll, insurance, household, warehouse, fence, front, laundering, fraud, payday, evidence, utility or legal-consequence engine is selected. These are compositions, content, derived views, boundaries or later choices as the rows specify. The [decision cards](OWNER_GOVERNANCE_DECISIONS.md) carry shared choices; the [conflict register](CONFLICT_RESOLUTIONS.md) carries all sixteen original groups. This mapping is documentary completeness, not evidence that every composition has passed an audit.
