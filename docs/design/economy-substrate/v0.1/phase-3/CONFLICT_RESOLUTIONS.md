## Selected defaults, rather than new decision gates

Economy can select the following now as part of its proposed design: five families; cash/bank form distinction; no new credit/FX system in the minimum slice; product/pool/consequential item distinction; inventory as view; ordinary service performance; related non-atomic commercial episodes; separate payment/transfer/fulfillment; cash-flow operating comparison; plural fallible books; optional off-books abstraction; declared external supply/consumer boundaries; constrained fence reuse; coarse/explicit single counting and prospective detail; no source cleansing or automatic detection.

Those selections do not require owner answers just to become reviewable. Shared activation/standing, legal coverage, capacity guarantees and chosen gameplay policies remain explicit D01-D11 recommendations. No unresolved documentary question is closed by inventing an API, event ordering, storage lifetime, normative authority or hidden knowledge.
